## Hydra farm (refined — RULES LOCKED in creatures.md; scene draft kept in hydra-containment-scene.md)

Severed heads regrow doubled. Team disables mobility, controls the
regeneration rate, and harvests teeth from dropped severed heads only —
never from the live beast. Pinned under too many heads, it cannot move
while they work. Body: armored scales, high physical and magical resistance; regen is
heads-only, and a true kill needs a heart strike — the team exploits
instead. Loop: cut → multiply → overload (slow body = easier cutting)
→ fallen heads collected → stop when loaded, never greedy → team
leaves → revert after days. Revert brings three heads with teeth
regenerated in base form; Edward bashes them out again (or knocks them
loose on his shield). No infinite abuse — each cycle needs skill and
timing. ~3 tons of teeth funded the party's gear. They believed it
could be farmed again.
Toothless hydra status: hidden away (possibly a sealed pouch in Xavier's
spatial bag). Role: secret weapon — tamed later, released by Xavier at
Ember while the heroes hold the enemy's strongest. Ties to Xavier's
locked Volume 3 taming hook ("wants a hydra as a pet").
"Dark Lord" is the author's working name for the top enemy — confirm at
lock whether this equals the corrupter mystery.

Cave origin: the Caldora nest was a golem cave first — broken golem
remains, mana cores eaten, hydra took over. It lured the party in and
blocked the escape: fight or die. Later they earned an escape chance and
chose greed — a trapped legendary means legendary loot at low risk, and
it barely hurts the beast.
Refined observations: more heads = slower but harder-hitting; heads
obstruct each other; shifting center mass wrecks balance — it steps to
keep from falling.
Eli's play: team stands back, she baits it alone, draws its mass
forward, tips it overreaching for her speed. Body grounded, heads
helpless — slash the overextended ones one by one. Its pride allows it:
it knows it regenerates.
Egg (LOCKED direction): they never captured the adult — they took an
egg and left, which is why it can't be used readily. No captured-hydra
problem, no instant war weapon; delayed payoff into a future emotional
and ethical arc (Xavier's tame). Egg-only retires the
hidden-toothless-adult and adult Ember-release locks (updated across
creatures.md, future-arcs, mentors hub). Scene's "Then we contain it"
ending still needs an egg beat. Hatching scene comes much later and
decides: ally, uncontrollable, or tragedy.
