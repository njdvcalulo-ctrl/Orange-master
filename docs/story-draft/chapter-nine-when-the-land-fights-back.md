# Orange: The Healer's Resolve — Volume 2: The Slime Crisis — Chapter Nine: When the Land Fights Back (Part 1)

The ground was wrong.

---

Orange felt it before he saw anything.

---

"…Something's off," he muttered.

---

Serra didn't look at him.

"…Explain."

---

Orange crouched.

Pressed his hand into the soil.

---

"…It's alive—but not healthy."

---

A pause.

---

"…It's reacting."

---

Serra's eyes narrowed slightly.

"…To what?"

---

Orange hesitated.

---

"…I don't know yet."

---

"Then find out."

---

---

They stood at the border.

---

The same place—

where everything started.

---

Where children once ran.

Where Amy fell.

Where Orange first healed.

---

Now—

it was quiet.

Too quiet.

---

"…No patrol?" Orange asked.

---

"There was," Serra replied.

---

"…Was?"

---

She pointed ahead.

---

"…Look."

---

A helmet.

Half-melted.

---

Orange's chest tightened.

---

"…Acid?"
---

"Stronger than before."

---

A pause.

---

"Stay sharp."

---

---

They moved forward.

---

Step by step.

---

The ground softened unnaturally.

---

"…This isn't just slime territory anymore," Orange said.

---

"…It's spreading."

---

Serra nodded.

"…Good."

---

Orange blinked.

"…Good?"
---

"You're thinking."

---

---

Then—

it moved.

---

Not a sound.

---

A shift.

---

Then—

*SPLOOSH.*

---

A slime dropped from above.

---

Bigger.

---

Much bigger.

---

"…That's not normal," Orange said.

---

"No," Serra replied calmly.

---

"…It's progress."

---

---

The slime lunged.

---

Orange drew his short sword.

---

"…Angle first," he muttered.

---

He stepped—

sideways.

---

The acid shot past him—

burning into the ground.

---

"…Too wide," he analyzed.

---

He closed the gap.

---

Strike—

---

The blade sank—

but not deep.

---

"…Density increased."

---

The slime pulsed.

---

Then—

split.

---

"…What?!"
---

Two now.

---

Serra didn't move.

---

"Adapt."

---

---

Orange exhaled sharply.

---

"…If cutting multiplies it…"

---

He shifted stance.

---

"…Then I need control—not force."

---

---

He stepped back.

---

Hand to the ground.

---

"…Grow."

---

The soil responded.

---

Roots surged upward—

wrapping one slime.

---

"…Hold it."

---

The second attacked.

---

Orange moved.

---

Short steps.

Efficient.

---

He waited—

timed—

---

Then struck the core.

---

Not wide.

---

Precise.

---

The slime collapsed.

---

The restrained one struggled—

then stilled.

---

---

Silence returned.

---

Orange stood still.

Breathing controlled.

---

"…So that's the difference," he said quietly.

---

Serra finally stepped forward.

---

"…Say it."

---

"…Before, I reacted."

---

A pause.

---

"…Now I observe first."

---

Serra nodded once.

---

"…Continue."

---

---

They moved deeper.

---

And what they found—

---

was worse.

---

The land was changing.

---

Plants—

wilting.

---

Water—

thickened.

---

And at the center—

---

a mass.

---

A cluster of slimes.

---

Fused.

---

Breathing.

---

"…That's not natural," Orange said.

---

"…No," Serra replied.

---

"…That's evolution."

---

---

The mass shifted.

---

Then—

multiple eyes opened.

---

"…It's aware."

---

Serra stepped forward.

---

"…Good."

---

Orange frowned.

---

"…You keep saying that."

---

"Because problems you can see—"

---

She drew her weapon.

---

"…can be solved."

---

---

The creature attacked.

---

Multiple acid streams—

fast—

wide—

---

Orange moved.

---

Dodged.

Calculated.

---

"…Too many angles…"

---

He clenched his jaw.

---

"…Then reduce them."

---

---

"Grow."

---

The field answered.

---

Thick roots rose—

forming barriers.

---

Acid struck—

but slowed.

---

"…Not enough…"

---

Serra stepped in.

---

Fast.

---

Striking.

---

Controlled.

---

"…Watch closely," she said.

---

Her movements—

minimal.

---

Every step—

purposeful.

---

Every strike—

decisive.

---

"…She's not fighting the whole thing…"

---

Orange realized.

---

"…She's breaking it apart."

---

---

"Then I support."

---

He placed his hand down again.

---

"…Grow."

---

Stronger this time.

---

The ground surged—

separating the mass.

---

Exposing cores.

---

"…Now!"

---

Serra moved.

---

One.

---

Two.

---

Three—

---

The creature collapsed.

---

---

Silence.

---

Heavy.

---

Real.

---

Orange stood there.

---

"…That wasn't just stronger…"

---

"…It was adapting."

---

Serra nodded.

---

"…So are you."

---

---

Orange looked at the land.

---

Damaged.

---

Struggling.

---

"…I can't just fight this."

---

He knelt.

---

"…I need to restore it."

---

---

"…Grow."

---

Gentler this time.

---

Not force.

---

Support.

---

The soil responded.

---

Slowly.

---

But truly.

---

---

Serra watched quietly.

---

Then—

smiled.

---

"…Now you understand."

---

---

Orange stood.

---

"…This isn't about killing monsters."

---

A pause.

---

"…It's about protecting what they're destroying."

---

---

Serra turned away.

---

"…Good."

---

"…Because this is only the beginning."

---

---

Far from them—

---

something deeper in the land—

---

moved.

---

Larger.

---

Watching.

---

Waiting.

---

---

And for the first time—

---

the healer—

---

stood on a battlefield…

---

not just to survive—

---

but to defend.

---

---

**End of Chapter 9**
