# Hydra Containment Scene (Draft — Carrot Party Era, Non-Canon Holding)

Author's corrected version. Raw material, not canon. Rules it
demonstrates are locked in docs/world/creatures.md ("Verdant Hydra").

---

The cave should have been empty.

That was the first mistake.

---

"This should be a golem den," Edward said quietly.

---

Broken stone limbs lay scattered across the cave floor.

---

Crushed.

Cracked.

Drained.

---

Serra crouched near one of the cores.

---

Empty.

---

"…Something killed them," she said.

---

A pause.

---

"…And stayed."

---

"…Too quiet," Elinore murmured.

She crouched near the entrance, fingers brushing the ground.

---

"No tracks," she added.

"…No droppings."

---

Edward frowned.

"…So?"

---

Elinore stood slowly.

---

"…Something lives here."

---

A pause.

---

"…And it doesn't leave."

---

---

Serra stepped forward, boots crunching over old bone fragments.

---

"…Stay close."

---

Xavier rolled his shoulder, a faint flame dancing across his fingers.

---

"…If it's alive, I'll find it."

---

"You will," Serra said calmly.

"…After it finds you."

---

A low sound echoed.

---

Not a roar.

Not a growl.

---

A shift.

---

The ground trembled.

---

Then—

---

It rose.

---

Three heads.

Massive.

Scaled.

Watching.

---

"…Hydra," Edward whispered.

---

The center head lunged.

---

"MOVE—!"

---

Elinore vanished.

Serra stepped forward—shield up—taking the impact head-on.

---

"Left side!" Xavier shouted.

---

Flame flashed—

---

A neck was severed.

---

Clean.

---

The head fell.

---

Then—

---

The wound pulsed.

---

"…No," Serra said.

---

Two heads burst from the cut.

---

"IT MULTIPLIES—!"

---

---

### REALIZATION

They didn't panic.

---

They adjusted.

---

"Elinore—pattern!"

"Three beats—then strike," she replied.

---

"Edward—hold!"

"Already am!"

---

Serra didn't attack.

---

She watched.

---

"…It reacts to pressure," she said.

---

Xavier cut again.

---

Another head split—

---

More grew.

---

Too many.

---

"…Stop cutting," Serra ordered.

---

Even in battle—

they listened.

---

"…We're feeding it."

---

---

### STRATEGY SHIFT

Edward slammed his shield forward.

---

"Then we control it."

---

Elinore's arrows pinned joints.

---

Not lethal.

---

Restricting.

---

Xavier lowered his flames slightly.

---

"…We can't kill it."

---

A breath.

---

"…Not like this."

---

"…Then we survive it."

---

Serra nodded.

---

"…We manage it."

---

A pause.

---

"…We study it."

---

---

### THE DISCOVERY

Time passed.

---

They observed:

- more heads = slower movement
- excess growth = loss of balance
- weight = instability

---

"…It's collapsing under itself," Xavier said.

---

Serra's eyes sharpened.

---

"…Then we force imbalance."

---

Xavier's eyes narrowed.

---

"…We're not trapped…"

---

A faint grin.

---

"…It is."

---

Edward grinned.

---

"…Now that's a fight."

---

"Elinore—don't—" Edward started.

---

Too late.

---

She moved.

---

Fast.

---

Deliberate.

---

Not attacking.

---

Luring.

---

The Hydra lunged—

---

all heads forward—

---

overextending—

---

weight shifting—

---

balance breaking—

---

"NOW!" she shouted.

---

The massive body tipped.

---

CRASH.

---

The cavern shook.

---

Heads flailed—

---

unable to lift the body—

---

necks exposed—

---

Xavier moved.

---

Blade flashing.

---

Cut.

---

Cut.

---

Cut.

---

"Take only what we can carry!" Serra ordered.

---

---

### THE FARM LOOP

They moved with precision.

---

Cut—

Step back—

Let it grow—

Let it strain—

---

Heads multiplied.

---

Body slowed.

---

Then—

---

"…There!" Edward shouted.

---

Elinore moved first.

---

Not to kill—

---

To collect.

---

Severed heads.

---

Still twitching.

---

"…Teeth are intact," she said.

---

Xavier raised a brow.

---

"…So we don't fight it…"

---

Edward smirked.

---

"…We harvest it."

---

---

### CONTROL, NOT VICTORY

They didn't stay long.

---

"…We can leave," Edward said.

---

No one moved.

---

Xavier looked at the fallen heads.

---

"…Or we don't."

---

They gathered what they could—

---

Then withdrew.

---

---

### THE RETURN

Days later—

they came back.

---

The Hydra remained.

---

Three heads.

---

Whole again.

---

Teeth restored.

---

Alive.

---

---

Edward stepped forward.

---

Shield raised.

---

The Hydra struck—

---

CLANG—

---

Teeth shattered against reinforced steel.

---

Fragments scattered.

---

"…That's one way to do it," Xavier said.

---

---

### THE DECISION

Silence settled.

---

"…We should kill it," Elinore said.

---

Straightforward.

---

Practical.

---

Serra stepped forward.

---

"…No."

---

"…Why?"

---

Serra looked at the Hydra.

---

Not as prey.

---

Not as enemy.

---

---

"…Because we understand it."

---

A pause.

---

"…And anything understood…"

---

Her gaze hardened.

---

"…can be used."

---

---

Edward exhaled.

---

"…We don't rely on it."

---

Xavier smirked.

---

"…We don't need to."

---

Elinore turned away.

---

"…Then we contain it."

---

---

Serra nodded.

---

"…And we decide when it becomes a weapon."

---

---

Deep in the cave—

---

The Hydra watched.

---

Learning.

---

Waiting.

### End Scene
