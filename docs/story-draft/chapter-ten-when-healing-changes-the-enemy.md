# Orange: The Healer's Resolve — Volume 2: The Slime Crisis — Chapter Ten: When Healing Changes the Enemy (Part 1)

The village did not wait.

---

"They're evolving."

---

Edward stood at the center of Ylila.

Calm.

Firm.

---

"Then we adapt faster."

---

Training began the same day.

---

Not just for Orange.

---

For everyone.

---

"Listen carefully," Edward said, voice carrying across the field.

---

"Slimes are not mindless."

---

"They react."

"They learn."

"And if you don't understand that—"

---

"You die."

---

No one laughed.

---

---

Groups were formed.

---

Farmers became runners.

Fishers became scouts.

Laborers became defenders.

---

And among them—

---

Orange's friends stood together.

---

Leo.

Amy.

Sasha.

---

"…We're really doing this," Sasha whispered.

---

Amy tightened her grip on the wooden blade.

"…We already did."

---

Leo cracked his knuckles.

"…This time, we don't run."

---

---

Edward stepped toward them.

---

"You three have already faced them once."

---

A pause.

---

"You lived."

---

"…Barely," Leo muttered.

---

Edward nodded.

---

"Good."

---

"…That means you know fear."

---

He turned.

---

"Now we teach you control."

---

---

Training was different for them.

---

Not just drills.

---

Not just movement.

---

They trained with Orange.

---

Again.

---

And again.

---

And again.

---

"Hold still," Orange said, placing his hand on Leo's arm.

---

Warmth flowed.

---

The bruises faded.

---

Leo flexed his hand.

---

"…Feels stronger."

---

Orange paused.

---

"…You said that before."

---

Amy nodded.

"…Yeah… every time you heal us… it's like we recover… better than before."

---

Silence.

---

Orange frowned.

---

"…That shouldn't happen."

---

Serra, watching nearby, spoke calmly.

---

"…Why not?"

---

Orange looked at her.

---

"…Healing should restore—not improve."

---

Serra crossed her arms.

---

"…That's your assumption."

---

A pause.

---

"…Test it."

---

---

They did.

---

Repeated training.

Repeated damage.

Repeated healing.

---

Small improvements.

---

Consistent.

---

Stacking.

---

"…This isn't normal," Orange said.

---

"…No," Serra replied.

---

"…It's potential."

---

---

Days passed.

---

The village grew stronger.

---

Not just physically—

---

but together.

---

---

Then—

the call came.

---

"North sector—movement!"

---

Orange stood instantly.

---

"…Let's go."

---

This time—

he wasn't alone.

---

Leo moved beside him.

Amy drew her blade.

Sasha climbed to higher ground.

---

Serra watched.

---

But did not lead.

---

"…Your call," she said.

---

Orange nodded.

---

"…We hold formation."

---

---

They reached the field.

---

Slimes.

---

More than before.

---

Bigger.

---

Faster.

---

"…Positions!" Orange called.

---

Leo stepped forward.

Shield up.

---

Amy flanked.

---

Sasha drew—

---

"Left side, three incoming!"

---

---

The fight began.

---

Leo held the line.

---

Acid struck—

---

He endured.

---

"…I can take it!"

---

Amy moved in—

clean strikes—

---

Precise.

---

"…They're softer at the core!"

---

Sasha's arrows followed—

---

Perfect timing.

---

---

Orange moved between them.

---

Healing.

Supporting.

---

Watching.

---

Thinking.

---

"…They're reacting to pressure…"

---

"…Then we control the pace."

---

---

A slime lunged—

faster than expected—

---

Leo staggered.

---

"…Too fast—!"

---

Orange reacted—

instinct—

---

Hand forward—

---

"…Heal!"

---

The warmth surged—

---

But something changed.

---

The slime—

froze.

---

Its body trembled.

---

The acid stopped.

---

"…What—?"
---

It didn't attack.

---

It… recoiled.

---

As if—

hurt.

---

"…I didn't damage it…"

---

Orange stepped back.

---

"…I healed it."

---

---

Silence in motion.

---

The slime pulsed weakly.

---

Then—

settled.

---

No aggression.

---

No attack.

---

It stayed—

still.

---

---

"…Did you just…" Amy started.

---

"…Pacify it?" Sasha finished.

---

Leo lowered his guard slightly.

---

"…Or confuse it…"

---

---

Orange stared.

---

"…No."

---

His voice sharpened.

---

"…Something's wrong."

---

---

He stepped closer.

---

Slow.

Careful.

---

The slime didn't move.

---

"…It's not attacking…"

---

"…But it's not normal either."

---

---

Serra finally spoke.

---

"…What did you feel?"
---

Orange didn't look away.

---

"…Resistance."

---

A pause.

---

"…Like something inside it didn't accept the healing."

---

---

Serra's eyes narrowed.

---

"…Poisoned?"
---

"…Or corrupted," Orange replied.

---

---

The slime shifted slightly.

---

Not aggressive.

---

But… aware.

---

---

"…It changed," Orange said quietly.

---

"…Because of you," Serra replied.

---

---

Silence.

---

Heavy.

---

---

"…That shouldn't be possible."

---

Orange clenched his fist.

---

"…Healing isn't supposed to do that."

---

---

Serra stepped beside him.

---

"…Then stop thinking about what it's 'supposed' to do."

---

---

A pause.

---

"…Start asking what it *can* do."

---

---

Orange looked at the slime again.

---

"…If it reacts differently…"

---

"…Then it's not just healing."

---

---

"…It's interaction."

---

---

The slime moved—

slightly—

---

But didn't attack.

---

---

Leo stepped back.

"…So what now?"
---

---

Orange exhaled slowly.

---

"…We don't kill it."

---

---

Amy blinked.

"…What?"

---

---

"…Not yet."

---

---

Sasha lowered her bow.

"…You're serious?"

---

---

Orange nodded.

---

"…We observe."

---

---

Serra smiled.

---

Faint.

---

"…Good."

---

---

Because this—

---

was no longer just a battle.

---

---

It was a discovery.

---

---

And somewhere deep within the corrupted land—

---

something stronger stirred.

---

---

If healing could change monsters—

---

then the question was no longer simple.

---

---

Were slimes enemies?

---

Or something else entirely?

---

---

**End of Chapter 10**
