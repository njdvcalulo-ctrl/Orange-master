# Chapter 8 (Beats)

News spreads of growing agriculture in Ylila village and nobles are getting greedy to get the land. News reaches Edward and preparations are done. As part of it, Xavier and Ellinore join in the training, finding Orange's technique with the farm tools weird — farmer, healer, and fighter?

Also new reports from the adventurers guild border patrol announce changing slime activity. It seems the slimes have evolved to be bigger slimes and can spit acid and eat vegetation rapidly. Needs help from guild.

Future idea: involve Orange's friends in training. Leo to be trained by Edward as tank, Amy as swordsman, and Sasha as archer.

# Draft Prose (Part 1)

**Orange: The Healer's Resolve**
**Chapter Eight: Seeds of Conflict**

News traveled faster than wagons.

---

Within weeks—

Ylila was no longer just a quiet farming village.

---

It was something else.

---

"…Unusual yield."
"…Rapid growth…"
"…Consistent surplus…"

---

"…Impossible."

---

But not impossible.

---

Just unseen before.

---

---

In distant halls—

nobles listened.

---

"…A small village?" one scoffed.

---

Another leaned forward.

"…Land like that… shouldn't be wasted on farmers."

---

A pause.

---

"…Send word."

---

Greed had taken root.

---

---

Back in Ylila—

Edward stood before the villagers.

---

"…We prepare."

---

No panic.

---

Only readiness.

---

"We don't know who will come. Or when."

---

His gaze swept across them.

---

"But we will not be unprepared."

---

---

Training intensified.

---

Not just for Orange.

---

But for the village.

---

And for the first time—

Serra wasn't alone.

---

Xavier stepped forward.

---

"…So this is the kid."

---

Orange stood still.

---

"…Yes, sir."

---

Xavier smirked.

---

"Don't call me that. Fight me instead."

---

"…What?"

---

Before he could react—

---

*CLANG.*

---

A wooden blade struck his hoe.

---

Orange staggered back.

---

"…Too slow," Xavier said.

---

Another strike.

---

Faster.

---

Stronger.

---

Orange barely blocked.

---

"…You hit like a farmer."

---

"…I am a farmer."

---

Xavier grinned.

---

"Then let's make that dangerous."

---

---

From the sidelines—

Ellinore watched.

---

Silent.

---

Then—

she vanished.

---

Orange blinked.

---

"…Where did—"

---

A whisper behind him.

---

"You're dead."

---

He froze.

---

Ellinore stood there.

---

"…You don't see threats," she said calmly.

---

"You react too late."

---

---

Serra crossed her arms.

---

"…Good."

---

Orange looked between them.

---

"…So I need to learn both?"
---

Serra nodded.

---

"Fight."

---

"See."

---

"Decide."

---

---

The training changed.

---

No longer just endurance.

---

Now—

combat.

---

Awareness.

---

Adaptation.

---

---

Meanwhile—

another report arrived.

---

From the Adventurers' Guild.

---

Edward read it slowly.

---

Then again.

---

"…This isn't normal."

---

Serra stepped beside him.

---

"…What is it?"
---

He handed her the report.

---

"…Border patrol."

---

She read.

---

Then frowned.

---

"…Slimes?"
---

"Not the same ones."

---

A pause.

---

"…They've grown."

---

"Bigger."

---

"More aggressive."

---

"…Acid stronger."

---

Serra's expression hardened.

---

"…And this—"

---

Edward pointed to the last line.

---

"…'Vegetation decay increasing.'"

---

---

The fields.

---

---

Serra exhaled slowly.

---

"…So it begins."

---

---

Orange stood in the distance.

Training.

Learning.

Growing.

---

Unaware—

that the very thing he had begun to nurture—

---

was now being threatened.

---

---

And far away—

---

eyes turned toward Ylila.

---

Not with gratitude.

---

But with hunger.

---

---

Because when something grows too well—

---

the world doesn't just notice.

---

---

It comes to take it.
