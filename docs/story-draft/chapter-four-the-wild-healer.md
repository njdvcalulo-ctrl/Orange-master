**Orange: The Healer's Resolve**
**Chapter Four: The Wild Healer**

Three days after the letter was sent—

it was answered.

---

Far from the quiet fields of Ylila, beyond roads worn by merchants and paths avoided by the fearful, a woman stood beneath the open sky.

A letter rested in her hand.

---

She read it once.

Then again.

---

A small smile formed—

barely there.

But real.

---

"…The future, huh…"

---

She folded the letter carefully.

Tucked it away.

And turned.

---

"Looks like we're making a detour."

---

Behind her stood two figures.

A swordsman draped in a worn red cape, his posture relaxed but ready—like a blade that never dulled.

And an archer, quiet and observant, eyes already scanning paths that had yet to be taken.

---

"…A village?" the swordsman asked.

---

"Yeah."

She stretched her shoulders lightly.

"…Let's see what kind of seed is growing there."

---

And without another word—

they moved.

---

Days later—

they arrived.

---

The village of Ylila stood as it always had.

Quiet.

Steady.

Unassuming.

---

Fields stretched wide under the sun—rice paddies reflecting the sky like mirrors of light. Rows of vegetables swayed gently with the wind. Coconut trees stood tall at the edges of the land, while beyond them, the great lake shimmered—home to fishermen who rose before dawn and returned with the day's provision.

---

It was a place that expected little—

and needed only enough.

---

But unseen by most—

something had already changed.

---

Orange worked in the fields as he always did.

Barefoot in the mud.

Hands steady.

Movements efficient.

---

To anyone watching—

he was just a farmer's son.

---

But beneath the surface—

something continued to grow.

---

"…Again."

He lifted the bundle.

Carried it.

Felt the strain—

then the response.

---

The warmth answered him.

Always.

---

He said nothing.

But he remembered.

---

A week had passed since the incident.

Since Amy.

Since *that moment*.

---

And then—

someone noticed.

---

"…Visitors."

---

Heads turned.

---

Rare.

Unusual.

---

In Ylila, people left.

They did not arrive.

---

Three figures approached from the distant path.

---

The first—

a woman.

---

Her steps were unhurried.

But deliberate.

---

There was nothing flashy about her.

No grand robes.

No glowing staff.

---

And yet—

no one could look away.

---

The second—

a swordsman in a red cape, resting his blade over his shoulder as if it weighed nothing.

---

The third—

an archer, silent as shadow, eyes already mapping the village before she stepped into it.

---

"…Adventurers…"

---

The word spread quickly.

---

By the time they reached the center—

Chief Edward was already waiting.

---

"…Serra."

---

The woman stopped.

Then smiled.

---

"Still alive, old man?"

---

Edward exhaled through his nose.

"…Barely."

---

The tension broke.

But only slightly.

---

They were welcomed.

Fed.

Then brought to the chief's office.

---

The door closed.

---

"…So."

Serra leaned back casually, one leg crossed over the other.

"…You dragged me all the way out here for a kid?"

---

Edward didn't react.

---

"…Not just a kid."

---

A pause.

---

"A healer."

---

Serra's eyes shifted.

Slightly.

---

"…That so."

---

Edward continued.

"No incantation. No formal training."

---

Now—

she was listening.

---

"…He healed another child."

---

Silence.

---

Then—

a small chuckle escaped her.

---

"…And you want me to what?"
---

Edward met her gaze.

---

"…Guide him."

---

Serra leaned forward.

Elbows on knees.

Eyes sharp now.

---

"…Or you want me to confirm if he's worth guiding?"

---

Edward didn't answer.

---

He didn't need to.

---

Serra grinned.

---

"There it is."

---

She stood.

Stretched lightly.

---

"…Alright."

---

Her voice carried a different tone now.

Lighter.

But edged.

---

"I'll take a look."

---

She turned toward the door.

---

"But let's be clear."

---

Her hand rested on the handle.

---

"I'm not here to babysit."

---

A glance back.

---

"If the kid doesn't have what it takes—"

---

"I walk."

---

The door opened.

---

"…We'll see how far he can go."

---

A pause.

Then—

a laugh.

---

"Hahahahaha—this might actually be interesting."

---

Outside—

the village continued its quiet rhythm.

---

Unaware—

that something had just arrived…

that would change one boy's life forever.

---

Serra walked through the fields.

Slow.

Observing.

---

Then she saw him.

---

A boy.

Covered in dirt.

Working.

---

No hesitation.

No complaint.

---

Just movement.

Effort.

Consistency.

---

"…Huh."

---

She stopped.

Watched.

---

Longer than necessary.

---

"…Not bad."

---

But her eyes narrowed slightly.

---

"…Let's see what you're really made of."

---

Because to Serra—

power was never the measure.

---

Character was.

---

Strength was not forcing the world to bend.

---

It was choosing to stand—

so others wouldn't fall.

---

It was lifting—

not crushing.

---

Guiding—

not controlling.

---

Protecting—

even when it cost you.

---

"…You can plant the seed," she murmured quietly.

"…You can water it. Tend it."

---

Her gaze remained on Orange.

---

"…But only God makes it grow."

---

A faint smile returned.

---

"…Let's see how you grow, kid."

---

And somewhere deep within Orange—

without him even knowing—

something responded.

---

Not to her presence.

---

But to what was coming.

---

Because the next lesson—

would not be gentle.

---

To Serra—

training was simple.

---

"If you can still stand comfortably—"

---

"Then you haven't started yet."

---

The Wild Healer had arrived.

---

And with her—

the true beginning of Orange's journey.
