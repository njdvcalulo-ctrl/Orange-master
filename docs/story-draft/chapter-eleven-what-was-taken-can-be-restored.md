# Orange: The Healer's Resolve — Volume 2: The Slime Crisis — Chapter Eleven: What Was Taken Can Be Restored (Part 1)

The slime didn't attack.

---

It stayed where it was.

---

Breathing.

Pulsing.

---

Alive—

but different.

---

Orange didn't move.

---

"…This isn't normal," he said.

---

Serra stood beside him.

"…Define normal."

---

Orange frowned.

---

"…Slimes attack."

"…They react to movement."

"…They don't… hesitate."

---

A pause.

---

"…This one did."

---

---

Leo shifted uneasily.

"…So what—it's broken?"

---

Amy tightened her grip.

"…Or waiting?"

---

Sasha didn't lower her bow.

"…Or pretending."

---

---

Orange stepped closer.

---

Slow.

Measured.

---

"…If it wanted to attack," he said quietly, "it already would have."

---

The slime trembled slightly.

---

But didn't strike.

---

---

"…When I healed it," Orange continued, "it resisted."

---

Serra glanced at him.

"…Explain."

---

"…It felt like…" he hesitated.

---

"…Like something inside it didn't belong."

---

Silence.

---

"…A foreign presence," Serra said.

---

Orange nodded.

---

"…Yes."

---

---

Leo frowned.

"…So you're saying…"

---

"…It was sick?" Amy finished.

---

---

Orange looked at them.

---

"…Not just sick."

---

A pause.

---

"…Changed."

---

---

Sasha lowered her bow slightly.

"…By what?"

---

---

Orange's expression hardened.

---

"…That's the real problem."

---

---

He turned back to the slime.

---

"…If something corrupted it…"

---

"…Then this isn't just a monster issue."

---

---

Serra smiled faintly.

---

"…Now you're thinking like a healer."

---

---

Orange stepped forward again.

---

"…Let's test it."

---

---

Leo blinked.

"…Test what?"

---

---

Orange knelt.

---

Slowly—

he extended his hand.

---

"…If I'm right…"

---

"…then healing doesn't just fix damage."

---

A breath.

---

"…It restores what was taken."

---

---

"…Orange," Amy said carefully, "if it attacks—"

---

"…Then we respond," he said calmly.

---

"…But if it doesn't—"

---

He looked at them.

---

"…Then we're looking at something new."

---

---

Serra didn't interfere.

---

She watched.

---

Closely.

---

---

Orange placed his hand on the slime.

---

"…Heal."

---

---

The warmth flowed.

---

Gentle this time.

---

Controlled.

---

Not forced.

---

---

The slime pulsed—

violently.

---

Then—

stilled.

---

---

A faint glow spread through its body.

---

The murky, unstable color—

---

began to clear.

---

---

"…It's changing…" Sasha whispered.

---

---

The slime shrank slightly.

---

Condensed.

---

Stabilized.

---

---

Then—

slowly—

---

it moved.

---

---

Toward Orange.

---

---

Leo stepped forward.

"…Hey—!"

---

---

"Wait," Orange said.

---

---

The slime stopped in front of him.

---

Not aggressive.

---

Not defensive.

---

---

It simply stayed.

---

---

"…It's… calm," Amy said.

---

---

Serra crossed her arms.

---

"…Not calm."

---

A pause.

---

"…Aligned."

---

---

Orange tilted his head slightly.

---

"…Aligned… to what?"

---

---

The slime shifted—

just a little—

---

closer.

---

---

"…To you," Serra said.

---

---

Silence fell.

---

---

"…That doesn't make sense," Leo muttered.

---

---

"…No," Orange replied.

---

"…It does."

---

---

They looked at him.

---

---

"…If it was corrupted…"

---

"…and the corruption was removed…"

---

"…then what's left is its natural state."

---

---

"…And its natural state is…?" Sasha asked.

---

---

Orange looked at the slime.

---

"…Undecided."

---

---

A pause.

---

---

"…Then it chooses," Serra said.

---

---

The slime pulsed once.

---

Soft.

---

---

And stayed beside Orange.

---

---

Leo exhaled slowly.

"…You've got to be kidding me…"

---

---

Amy blinked.

"…Did we just… make friends with a slime?"

---

---

Sasha gave a small, uncertain laugh.

"…That's not something I thought I'd say today…"

---

---

Orange didn't smile.

---

---

"…This isn't about making friends."

---

---

He looked at the land.

---

Damaged.

---

Struggling.

---

---

"…This is about understanding what's really happening."

---

---

Serra nodded.

---

"…Good."

---

---

Because this—

---

was bigger than them.

---

---

Far beyond the northern fields—

---

hidden from sight—

---

something moved.

---

Not a slime.

---

Not a beast.

---

---

But something that watched.

---

---

And learned.

---

---

"…So," Leo said slowly.

"…What now?"

---

---

Orange stood.

---

The slime remained at his side.

---

---

"…Now?"

---

---

His grip tightened on his sword.

---

---

"…We save the rest."

---

---

A pause.

---

---

"…And then…"

---

---

His eyes hardened.

---

---

"…we find out who did this."

---

---

Because if something could corrupt life—

---

then it could do worse.

---

---

And if healing could restore it—

---

then Orange's role was no longer small.

---

---

It was necessary.

---

---

And soon—

---

others would come.

---

---

Not to understand.

---

---

But to take.

---

---

The nobles had heard of Ylila.

---

---

And they were not coming to help.

---

---

They were coming—

---

for everything.

---

---

**End of Chapter 11**
