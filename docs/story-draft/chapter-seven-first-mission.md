# Draft Prose (Part 1)

**Orange: The Healer's Resolve**
**Chapter Seven: The First Mission**

The barns were full.

Too full.

---

Sacks of rice were stacked to the ceiling.

Crates of vegetables lined the walls.

Even the outer storage sheds had begun to overflow.

---

"…We can't keep all of this," one of the farmers muttered.

---

At the center of it all stood Chief Edward.

Arms crossed.

Thinking.

---

"…We move it," he decided.

---

"To where?" someone asked.

---

Edward's gaze lifted.

Toward the distant road.

---

"…The City of Ember."

---

A murmur spread.

---

The nearest city-castle.

Ruled by the Sebastians.

A place of trade.

Of power.

---

And recently—

of trouble.

---

"They're running low on food," Edward continued. "Word reached us last week."

---

Silence.

---

"…Then this isn't just trade," one elder said.

---

Edward nodded.

---

"It's timing."

---

And timing—

meant responsibility.

---

---

Preparations began immediately.

---

Wagons were reinforced.

Supplies packed carefully.

Routes reviewed.

---

And for the first time—

Orange stood among them.

---

"…You're coming," Serra said.

---

He blinked.

"…Me?"

---

"You wanted to learn."

---

A pause.

---

"Then learn outside the field."

---

---

The morning of departure arrived.

---

John and Marie stood beside the wagon.

---

Orange adjusted the strap on his shoulder.

---

"…Be careful," Marie said, placing a hand on his cheek.

---

"I will."

---

John gave a short nod.

---

"Don't rely only on your strength."

---

Orange smiled faintly.

"…I won't."

---

---

The caravan moved.

---

The road stretched long and uneven.

Fields faded into wild land.

---

Serra walked ahead.

Xavier rested his blade across his shoulder.

Ellinore moved quietly along the edges—watching.

---

Orange stayed close to the wagon.

Observing.

Learning.

---

"…Stay alert," Serra said without turning.

---

"I am."

---

"Good."

---

---

Hours passed.

---

The air shifted.

---

Ellinore stopped.

---

"…Movement."

---

Everything slowed.

---

Serra raised a hand.

---

"…Positions."

---

Xavier stepped forward.

Serra moved to the center.

Ellinore vanished.

---

Orange's heart pounded.

---

"…What do I do?" he asked.

---

Serra glanced back.

---

"…Don't die."

---

Then—

they came.

---

Goblins.

---

Small.

Fast.

Ugly.

---

They rushed from the brush—

weapons crude but deadly.

---

Orange froze—

just for a moment.

---

Then remembered—

---

*Move.*

---

The goblins rushed in—

fast.

Ugly.

Screaming.

---

Orange froze.

His hands shook.

"…I—what do I do?!"

---

Serra didn't move.

Didn't step in.

---

Instead—

she threw something at him.

---

"Catch."

---

Orange fumbled—

then caught it.

---

A hoe.

---

"…What?"

---

A goblin lunged.

Closer.

Closer—

---

"ORANGE!"

---

Serra's voice cut through everything.

---

"WHAT DO YOU DO IN THE FIELD?!"

---

"…I—"

---

"LOUDER!"

---

"…I FARM!"

---

"THEN FARM!"

---

The goblin leapt—

---

"PLOW!"

---

Orange swung—

instinctively—

---

*CRACK.*

---

The hoe struck the goblin's head.

It dropped instantly.

---

Silence—

for half a second.

---

Orange blinked.

"…That worked?!"

---

Another goblin charged.

---

Serra didn't hesitate.

---

"TURN THE SOIL!"

---

Orange twisted his body—

low swing—

---

*THUD.*

---

Second goblin down.

---

"…WHAT?!"

---

"AGAIN!"

---

A third rushed in.

---

"PLANT!"

---

Orange thrust forward—

the wooden end drove into its chest—

---

It flew back.

---

Collapsed.

---

Now—

he was breathing hard.

---

But moving.

---

Not thinking.

---

Doing.

---

"WATER THE FIELD!"

---

Orange stepped forward—

wide arc—

---

The hoe swept through two at once—

---

They fell.

---

"…THIS IS JUST FARMING!"

---

Serra smirked.

---

"EXACTLY."

---

The goblins weren't prepared.

---

Not for a boy—

---

Not for a farmer—

---

Not for someone who had turned labor…

---

…into combat.

---

Orange stepped back.

Breathing hard.

---

"…I didn't—"

---

"You trained."

---

Serra's voice cut in.

---

She moved past him—

effortless.

---

"Don't think too much."

---

Another goblin charged.

---

This time—

Orange moved with purpose.

---

He didn't hesitate.

---

Didn't overthink.

---

He acted.

---

And one by one—

they fell.

---

Not because he was trying to overpower them—

---

But because—

---

he already had.

---

---

Silence returned.

---

Ellinore reappeared.

"…Clear."

---

Xavier exhaled.

"…Not bad, kid."

---

Orange looked at his hands.

---

"…I didn't feel that strong before…"

---

Serra crossed her arms.

---

"You were wearing weights."

---

A pause.

---

"You got used to being weak."

---

Orange blinked.

---

"…So this is—"

---

"Your actual body."

---

---

The journey continued.

---

And this time—

Orange walked differently.

---

Not proud.

---

But aware.

---

---

The City of Ember rose before them.

---

Tall walls.

Burning torches.

Crowds at the gates.

---

But something felt off.

---

The people—

looked tired.

---

Shops were open—

but lacking.

---

Food stalls—

nearly empty.

---

"…They weren't exaggerating," Xavier muttered.

---

---

At the merchant hall—

negotiations began.

---

A well-dressed man stepped forward.

---

"…We appreciate your effort," he said smoothly. "But as you can see, supply has stabilized."

---

Serra said nothing.

---

"…We can offer this price."

---

Low.

Too low.

---

Some of the farmers hesitated.

---

It sounded official.

---

Convincing.

---

"…No."

---

Serra's voice cut through.

---

Calm.

Sharp.

---

The merchant blinked.

"…Excuse me?"
---

She stepped forward.

---

"If supply was stable—your people wouldn't look like that."

---

A pause.

---

She gestured outside.

---

"Your stalls are empty."

"Your buyers are desperate."

---

Her eyes locked onto his.

---

"You don't need negotiation."

---

"You need this."

---

Silence.

---

Then—

the man smiled.

---

"…You're not from here."

---

Serra smiled back.

---

"…Exactly."

---

A longer pause.

---

Then—

"…Name your price."

---

---

The deal was made.

---

Fair.

Right.

---

Enough to help both sides.

---

---

By the time they left—

the wagons were empty.

---

But their return—

was full.

---

---

The road home felt lighter.

---

Not just from the weight—

---

but from purpose.

---

---

When they arrived—

the village celebrated.

---

Food was shared.

Stories told.

Laughter filled the night.

---

And Orange—

sat quietly for a moment.

---

Looking at his hands.

---

Then at the people around him.

---

"…I helped…"

---

Not perfectly.

---

Not completely.

---

But truly.

---

---

Serra sat beside him.

---

"…First mission," she said.

---

Orange nodded.

---

"…I was scared."

---

"Good."

---

"…But I still moved."

---

Serra smiled faintly.

---

"That's what matters."

---

---

Orange looked up at the stars.

---

"…There's more out there, isn't there?"
---

Serra followed his gaze.

---

"…A lot more."

---

---

And for the first time—

---

the road ahead didn't feel distant.

---

It felt—

---

real.

---

---

The healer had taken his first step beyond the village.

---

And the world—

had begun to open.
