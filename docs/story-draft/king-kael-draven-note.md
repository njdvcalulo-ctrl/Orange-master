# King Kael Draven — Final Battle Design (NOT CANON — Volume 2+, parked)

Author-approved build, parked until Volume 1 (fortress Ylila) is done.
Do not use in Volume 1. Decides the Ember war's end, not Ylila's defense.

## Kael Draven, King of Ember (proposed)

Ex-Dark Knight. Took the throne by killing the old king alongside his
generals, to sort the broken system by force. Late 40s, human.
Knight → Black Knight → Sovereign Knight (unique).
Calm, resolute, unshakable; carries every life lost under his command;
touches his chest (old wound) when thinking.
Goal: never let the kingdom starve again.
Belief: "A king who hesitates… buries his people."
Philosophy: not evil — sacrifice is necessary, some must suffer for many
to live, leadership means choosing who pays. Mirrors Orange's triage
from the other side.

Abilities (system-consistent): Burden Transfer (unique — redistributes
damage taken across nearby enemies, allies, environment); Black Guard
(channeling barrier, damage into stamina loss); Judgment Cleave (STR+AGI
wide-area); King's Domain (passive — boosts allies, suppresses weaker
enemies). Weakness: cannot protect everyone at once; breaks under
multiple simultaneous priorities; overextension opens him.

## Final battle shape (proposed triage battle)

Not a duel, not a power-up win, never Orange solo. Civilians present,
allies injured, team stretched. Orange heals, buffs, and chooses who to
save. Leo holds. Amy opens. Lyra lands precision. Slimes sustain.
Turning point: Orange accepts he cannot save everyone — and prioritizes.
Win condition: overload Burden Transfer with too many targets and
variables until it fails. Team-combined finish; Orange supports, never
deals the deciding blow out of anger.

Final moment: the King looks at Orange — "You chose… who to save."
Orange doesn't celebrate.

## Aftermath tones (both filed, B preferred outward)

A (realistic): victory celebrated, losses present, team exhausted —
some say "you could have saved more." Quiet pressure on Orange. High
expectations as a stressor: people assume power means everyone safe, as
if resources and hands were limitless.
B (healthier, preferred): grief and celebration together, team holding
each other, friends saying "you did what you could," family anchoring him.
Suggested blend: B outward (village celebrates and mourns well), A
inward (Orange carries the quiet unfair weight) — triage's cost, not
triumph.
