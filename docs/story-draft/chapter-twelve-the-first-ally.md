# Orange: The Healer's Resolve — Volume 2: The Slime Crisis — Chapter Twelve: The First Ally (Part 1)

The slime stayed.

---

That alone changed everything.

---

"…It's still following you," Leo muttered.

---

Orange didn't look back.

"…I know."

---

They moved deeper into the northern field.

---

This time—

not as hunters.

---

But as observers.

---

---

More slimes appeared.

---

From the ground.

From beneath roots.

From shallow water.

---

Dozens.

---

Amy tightened her grip.

"…That's a lot."

---

Sasha drew her bow.

"…Say the word."

---

---

"…Wait," Orange said.

---

The slimes didn't attack.

---

They gathered.

---

Watching.

---

Not hostile.

---

Not passive.

---

---

"…They're… responding," Orange said.

---

Serra stepped forward slightly.

---

"…To what?"

---

---

The small slime beside Orange pulsed.

---

Soft.

---

---

And the others…

---

answered.

---

---

A low ripple spread through them.

---

Not sound.

---

Not movement.

---

Something else.

---

"…Communication?" Sasha whispered.

---

---

Orange narrowed his eyes.

---

"…No…"

---

"…Recognition."

---

---

Then—

the ground shifted.

---

Not violently.

---

But deliberately.

---

---

Something larger rose.

---

---

A massive slime.

---

Twice the height of a man.

---

Its form—

unstable.

---

Heavy.

---

Dense.

---

---

"…That's new," Leo said under his breath.

---

---

Serra didn't move.

---

"…Careful."

---

---

Orange stepped forward.

---

"…If this is their center…"

---

"…then this is where it started."

---

---

The large slime pulsed.

---

Stronger than the rest.

---

But unstable.

---

---

"…It's still corrupted," Orange said.

---

---

The smaller slime beside him shifted—

closer—

as if urging him.

---

---

"…You want me to heal it?"
---

---

It pulsed again.

---

---

Serra spoke quietly.

---

"…This is your decision."

---

---

Orange didn't answer.

---

He stepped forward.

---

Slow.

---

Measured.

---

---

"…If this fails…"

---

"…we fight."

---

---

Leo stepped in.

"…Then we're ready."

---

Amy nodded.

---

Sasha adjusted her aim.

---

---

Orange reached out.

---

Placed his hand on the massive form.

---

---

"…Heal."

---

---

The response was immediate.

---

Violent.

---

---

The slime convulsed.

---

Its body surged—

unstable—

collapsing—

reforming—

---

---

"…Something's resisting harder…" Orange muttered.

---

---

He clenched his jaw.

---

"…Then I push deeper."

---

---

"…No," Serra said sharply.

---

---

Orange froze.

---

---

"…Don't overpower it."

---

---

A pause.

---

---

"…Understand it."

---

---

Orange exhaled.

---

---

"…Right."

---

---

He shifted.

---

---

"…God…"

---

"…If this life was altered…"

---

"…restore what You made it to be."

---

---

The warmth changed.

---

---

Not force.

---

Not pressure.

---

---

Invitation.

---

---

The slime trembled—

then—

---

stilled.

---

---

Silence fell.

---

---

Then—

light.

---

---

A faint glow emerged from its core.

---

---

"…What is that…" Amy whispered.

---

---

The glow intensified.

---

Clear.

---

Stable.

---

---

The murky corruption—

broke.

---

---

The massive slime collapsed inward—

---

shrinking—

---

condensing—

---

---

Then—

it reformed.

---

---

Smaller.

---

But denser.

---

More defined.

---

---

And then—

---

it spoke.

---

"…I…"

---

Everyone froze.

---

---

"…remember."

---

---

Leo stepped back.

"…No way…"

---

---

The slime shifted—

focusing—

---

"…pain…"

---

"…noise…"

---

"…not mine…"

---

---

Orange stared.

---

"…You were controlled."

---

---

“…yes…”

---

---

A pause.

---

---

"…you… removed it."

---

---

Silence.

---

---

"…You can think now," Sasha said quietly.

---

---

"…I can… choose."

---

---

Serra's eyes narrowed slightly.

---

"…Evolution through restoration…"

---

---

The slime turned—

toward the others.

---

---

"…we… followed…"

---

"…but did not know why…"

---

---

The smaller slimes gathered closer.

---

---

"…now… we know…"

---

---

It turned back to Orange.

---

---

"…you gave… back…"

---

A pause.

---

---

"…us."

---

---

Orange stood still.

---

---

"…Then what will you do with it?"

---

---

The slime pulsed once.

---

---

"…protect."

---

---

A pause.

---

---

"…with you."

---

---

Silence.

---

---

Leo exhaled.

"…We just gained… an army…"

---

---

Serra shook her head slightly.

---

"…Not an army."

---

---

She looked at Orange.

---

---

"…Allies."

---

---

The slime shifted.

---

---

"…I am… king."

---

---

Amy blinked.

"…Of… slimes?"
---

---

"…of those who follow…"

---

---

The others pulsed in response.

---

---

"…Slime King," Sasha whispered.

---

---

The king turned back to Orange.

---

---

"…we cooperate."

---

---

Orange nodded slowly.

---

---

"…Then we stand together."

---

---

A new bond was formed.

---

Not by force.

---

Not by fear.

---

---

But by restoration.

---

---

**End of Chapter 12**
