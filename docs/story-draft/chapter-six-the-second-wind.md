# Orange: The Healer's Resolve — Chapter Six: The Second Wind (Part 1)

The next morning—

Orange woke up before Serra called him.

---

His body still ached.

Every muscle protested.

Every movement reminded him of yesterday—

of breaking.

---

But something was different.

---

He sat up slowly.

Closed his eyes.

---

"…It's quieter."

---

The warmth was still there.

But no longer rushing.

No longer reacting in panic.

---

It was… steady.

---

Like a calm river.

---

Outside—

the fields waited.

---

Serra was already there.

---

"…You're early," she said without looking at him.

---

Orange nodded.

"I couldn't sleep long."

---

"Good."

---

She turned.

Tossed him a tool.

---

"No running today."

---

Orange blinked.

"…What?"

---

"Work."

---

He looked at the fields.

Then back at her.

---

"…That's it?"
---

Serra raised an eyebrow.

---

"You think you understand it already?"
---

"…No."

---

"Then stop talking."

---

---

The work began.

---

Not faster.

Not heavier.

---

But… intentional.

---

"Feel the soil."

---

Orange paused.

"…What?"

---

Serra sighed.

Walked over.

Knelt down.

---

She pressed her hand into the mud.

---

"Farming isn't just labor."

---

She looked at him.

---

"It's partnership."

---

"With what?" Orange asked.

---

"…Life."

---

She stood.

---

"You prepare the ground."

"You plant the seed."

"You water it."

---

A pause.

---

"But you don't make it grow."

---

Orange froze.

---

"…God does."

---

Serra nodded once.

---

"Good. You're listening."

---

---

They worked in silence after that.

---

But this time—

Orange didn't just move.

---

He paid attention.

---

To the soil.

Its weight.

Its moisture.

Its resistance.

---

To the water.

How it flowed.

Where it gathered.

Where it lacked.

---

To the plants.

Small.

Fragile.

Alive.

---

And slowly—

very slowly—

---

he felt something new.

---

Not inside him.

---

But outside.

---

"…Serra."

---

She didn't look at him.

"…What."

---

"…There's something…"

---

"…Yes."

---

She finally turned.

---

"Don't force it."

---

"Just observe."

---

---

Orange knelt beside a small patch of newly planted rice.

---

Closed his eyes.

---

The warmth within him—

didn't move.

---

Instead—

he reached.

---

Not physically.

---

But… intentionally.

---

Toward the plant.

---

Toward the soil.

---

Toward—

life.

---

"…Grow."

---

The word slipped out.

Soft.

Uncertain.

---

For a moment—

nothing happened.

---

Then—

---

A faint shimmer.

---

The soil shifted slightly.

The plant straightened—

just a little.

---

But it was enough.

---

Orange's eyes widened.

---

"…It responded…"

---

Serra watched closely.

---

"…Again."

---

This time—

he understood.

---

He didn't push.

Didn't force.

---

He aligned.

---

"…Grow."

---

The warmth flowed—

not into himself—

---

but through him.

---

Into the ground.

---

The plant strengthened.

Roots settled deeper.

Leaves brightened.

---

Still small.

Still natural.

---

But undeniably—

helped.

---

---

Orange pulled back.

Breathing slightly heavier.

---

"…I didn't heal it…"

---

Serra shook her head.

---

"No."

---

"You supported it."

---

A pause.

---

"That's your next step."

---

---

Days passed.

---

Training continued.

---

Running returned.

Weights increased.

Fieldwork intensified.

---

But now—

something had changed.

---

Orange didn't just recover.

---

He managed.

---

He paced.

---

He listened.

---

To his body.

To the work.

To the world around him.

---

And when he reached exhaustion—

---

he didn't panic.

---

He adjusted.

---

---

One afternoon—

Serra pushed him again.

---

"Full field."

---

Orange looked up.

---

"…All of it?"
---

"Before sunset."

---

"…That's impossible."

---

Serra smiled.

---

"Good."

---

---

He started.

---

Plow.

Carry.

Plant.

Water.

---

Again.

---

Again.

---

Again—

---

His body began to fail.

---

But this time—

he didn't just rely on healing.

---

He shifted.

---

Used less.

Moved smarter.

Rested in motion.

---

And when he couldn't go further—

---

he knelt.

---

Placed his hand on the ground.

---

"…Grow."

---

The field responded—

not instantly—

not overwhelmingly—

---

but enough.

---

The work eased.

---

Not removed.

---

Shared.

---

---

By sunset—

Orange stood.

---

The field—

done.

---

Not perfectly.

---

But fully.

---

---

Serra approached slowly.

---

Looked at the field.

Then at him.

---

"…You didn't brute force it."

---

Orange shook his head.

---

"I couldn't."

---

"…Good."

---

A small nod.

---

"That means you're learning."

---

---

Orange looked at his hands.

---

Still shaking.

Still tired.

---

But not empty.

---

"…I thought strength meant doing more…"

---

Serra crossed her arms.

---

"It does."

---

A pause.

---

"But not alone."

---

---

She turned away.

---

"Your healing isn't just for fixing damage."

---

"It's for sustaining life."

---

"For others."

---

"For the world."

---

---

Orange looked across the field.

---

The plants swayed gently in the wind.

---

Alive.

---

"…Then I'll learn to give it better."

---

---

Serra smiled faintly.

---

"…You will."

---

---

That night—

for the first time—

---

Orange slept without collapse.

---

Not because he wasn't tired—

---

but because he had learned—

---

how to continue.

---

---

And deep within him—

---

his power changed.

---

Not louder.

---

Not stronger.

---

But wiser.

---

---

A new skill had taken root.

---

Not born from force—

---

but from understanding.

---

---

**Grow.**

---

And with it—

---

the second wind had begun.
