# Volume 1 Outline (Locked)

## Act 1 — Foundation

Introduce village life, daily routines, relationships. Hint small irregularities (slimes, crops, injuries).

## Act 2 — Disruption

Minor problems increase. Team trains seriously. First real combat struggles. Healing limits exposed.

## Act 3 — Pressure

Threats coordinated or frequent. Resources strained. Villagers worry. Orange discovers deeper healing potential.

## Act 4 — Defense of Ylila

Organized attack or crisis. Teamwork required. No one strong enough alone. Orange calls priority, Leo holds, others strike.

## Act 5 — Resolution

Ylila survives but changed. Defense, training, farming improve. Orange earns recognition as essential, not strongest.
