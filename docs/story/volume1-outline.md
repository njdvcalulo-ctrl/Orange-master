# VOLUME 1: "Seeds of Resolve" (REVISED — LOCKED)

Theme: Protection begins at home.
End State: Ylila → Fortified Village (proto-stronghold).

Timeline rule (LOCKED): main story kids are 12–13 (Orange 12–13, Leo 13, Amy 12–13, Lyra 13). Younger selves (ages 8–10) appear only in flashbacks.

## Core Focus

Introduce world systems, build characters, establish Ylila identity, first conflict with Ember, unlock Slime System (SAS). End with preparation, not victory.

## Chapter 1 Opening (Locked: B)

Dream first, then farm life. Opens on a mysterious symbolic dream (burning field restored, "What you refuse to tend will be lost"), then wakes to meaningful farm life: Orange playful and restless, subtly dissatisfied with simple life, first hints of healing instinct and dream connection. No exposition, no systems lecture.

## Final Arc

Initial Ember Pressure: trade tension, small conflicts, scouting parties and minor clashes. Not full invasion yet.

Trigger Event: first real attack / raid — not overwhelming, but serious. Justifies fear, urgency, unity.

Mentor Involvement: Serra and possibly others step in — not to solve everything, but to teach response. Triage begins (light version), leadership starts forming.

Slime System Awakening: happens under pressure — accidental → emotional → reactive. Not OP: adaptive support, survival boost.

## Fortification Arc (Endgame)

Ylila transforms from peaceful farming village to organized defensive settlement.

Defense structure: wooden walls (early stage), basic watch towers, reinforced gates, terrain advantage (fields + forest edge).

Functional systems: food storage, watch rotations, signal system (horns, fires).

Strategic identity: not strongest, not richest — but efficient, cooperative, adaptive.

## Ember Delay (Locked)

Ember MUST delay full invasion, or it looks stupid. Internal problems:

1. Civil Unrest — protests ongoing, guards stretched.
2. Resource Allocation Conflict — food vs war supply, Darius vs Rizla tension.
3. Internal Power Balance — generals not fully aligned, each with own agenda.
4. King's Restraint — he doesn't rush. "A rushed war loses before it begins."

Result: Ember delays full invasion, sends probes and small attacks instead. Ylila gets time to grow.

## Ylila Identity (Locked)

Farmland → safe haven for people AND monsters. But controlled: "Controlled Harmony Zone" — humans + select monsters coexist, not all monsters allowed, requires effort + understanding. Unlike slime stories: not automatic unity, not instant loyalty. Trust is earned, danger still exists, mistakes happen.

## Orange's Role in Volume 1

NOT a hero savior. IS an emerging support core. Learns healing, learns limits, begins leadership, starts understanding responsibility. Doesn't carry everything yet.

Mentor rule: mentors do not win the battle — they enable the next generation. Final scenes: Serra guiding (not solving), Orange deciding, team acting together.

## End of Volume 1

Scene feel: walls standing, people tired, village changed. Tone is not "We won" but "We're ready." Final image: Orange on the wall, looking outward — not inward anymore.
