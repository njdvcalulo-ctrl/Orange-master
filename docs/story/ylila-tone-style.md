# Ylila Tone & Style Sheet (Locked)

One page. Action-packed but light. Kids can read it and have fun.

## Promise

Naruto-level heart. Sad but not traumatizing. Hard bans stay: no self-harm, no sexual content, no gore porn.
Faith stays subtle and grateful. Modest outfits only. No Campfire-style goddess gag.

> Strength is built, not given — and even the strongest still need others.

## Core loop

Strain outside → return to Ylila → rest / meal → process → go out again.

Every arc follows it. Every fight pays back into it. Mango Fiestival, Fishing Day, Blacksmith Day are not filler. They are why we fight.

## Fight rules: Slime-style clean action (Locked)

No blood pools. No innards. No torn bodies on page.

- Small foes poof into light, dissolve into mana, or drop as clean cuts.
- Big foes stay intact. Wounds read as cracks, scorch, dents, sleep.
- Kills happen. Camera looks away. Sound over sight: thud, pulse, hush.
- Nochi stores and splits. Slimes ferry. Harvest reads as food, not corpse.
- Aftermath is triage, not autopsy. Bandage, broth, rest station.
- Language: "down," "out," "stilled," "returned." Never splatter words.

Orange can fight and kill but is not the damage dealer. He makes winning possible.

## Why support carries: A-rank rule

Yuke works because the glue was invisible. Use that.

Leo holds. Amy cuts. Lyra finds. Orange times the heal, calls the shift, extends the window. Victory line always names the team.

## Work is cool: Piaro rule

Farming, cooking, repair, tanking, defense are finishers, not jokes.

Piaro farms and out-fights a Sword Saint. Orange cooks and the team evolves. Let process become technique. Skill Evolution must feel earned.

## Found family: Tempest rule

Start small. Befriend. Build.

Slimes are citizens and friends of Ylila, not pets or summons. Goblins-to-city pride applies here: huts to haven. Edward names them. King answers with light only.

## Cozy competence: Campfire rule

Food is joy. Meals are rest mechanics.

Marie's adobo, mango juice, grilled lake fish. Cooking scenes are slow on purpose. Smell, steam, passing bowls. Then back to danger refreshed.

## Comedy: Bofuri rule

Cute chaos, never cruel.

Maple breaks the game by accident. Ylila kids break expectations by effort. Leo fails a lift. Amy shows off and slips. Nochi wobbles with too many baskets. Devs-stress equivalent: Elder Ruel sighs, Teresa charges extra.

## Growth: Mushoku lesson, Ylila fix

Rudeus works and the world stays bigger than him. Keep that scale. Drop the fanservice and edge.

No spikes. No chosen one. Single-digit stats climb slowly. World humbles hero. Volume 1 stays base classes only.

## Do / Don't

Do: intact bodies, clean meat, triage talk, shared meals, earned cheers.
Don't: blood detail, innards, sexy gags, lone-wolf wins, instant power.

When in doubt, cut to Ylila: water, shade, first aid, hymn humming from the lake.
