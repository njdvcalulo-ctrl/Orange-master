# System Rules (Locked)

## 1. Everyone Has Access to Status

People can check their own status window. It shows level, stats, skills. This is normal, not special.

## 2. Growth Is Effort-Based

People improve through repetition, work, training, experience.

- No random power spikes.
- No chosen one advantage.

## 3. Skills Are Learned, Not Given

Skills come from habits, environment, influence, discipline.

Examples: farming → endurance. Cooking → nourishment. Crafting → precision.

## 4. Passive vs Active Skills

Passive skills: always active, subtle effects, built from lifestyle.

Active skills: require intent, consume energy, improve with use.

## 5. Core Theme Rule

> Power does not come from talent alone. Power comes from process.

## Unique System — Orange Core System

> Food ≠ Power. Process = Power.

Orange's cooking carries a growth signal: consistent eating + bonding + fighting alongside triggers adaptation (SAS — Symbiotic Adaptation System, FINAL). Requires intent, care, consistency, relationship. Cannot be mass-produced, stolen, or replicated by enemies. Catchphrase: "…Maybe it's because of the food." SAS upgrade (LOCKED): slimes transfer mana to their bonded user, redistribute mana during combat, stabilize mana flow — at will while attached. Applications: Leo sustains tanking longer, Lyra maintains high-focus shots, Amy extends combat duration, Orange amplifies support capacity. LIMIT: transfer is not infinite — slimes have limits (fatigue, cooldown). Keeps the system fair early.

Orange's future system comes from farming (input), cooking (process), healing (output).

Buffs are not from recipes alone. They require intent, care, consistency, relationship.

## Slime System (World Rule)

Slimes are low-tier creatures, adaptable, capable of evolving through environment, bonding, feeding.

- Not all slimes become powerful.
- Evolution depends on interaction and growth.

## Combat Philosophy

Fights are grounded, tactical, team-based. Positioning matters. Timing matters. Roles matter.

Avoid: flashy overpowering attacks, instant wins, unrealistic scaling.

Focus: teamwork, reading movement, exploiting openings, sustaining momentum.

## Family Influence Rule

Characters are shaped by parents, environment, daily life. Skills must reflect upbringing. Passives must feel natural.

## Skill Evolution (World Rule — Locked)

HOW you do the work shapes WHAT the skill becomes. Repeat a craft with your own style long enough — dancing while you slash, humming while you forge — and the skill can evolve into a new form (e.g., sword dance: effective, efficient slicing while dancing). Evolution requires correct, repeated practice; style alone without discipline evolves nothing. Evolved skills stay within the person's lane — no leaps into unrelated powers.
