# Conflict Engine + Progression (Locked)

## Core Conflict Engine

Ylila exists in fragile balance. Nearby threats: wandering monster groups, unstable ecosystems, external forces (traders, soldiers, unknown factions).

> Problems grow if ignored.

Small issues escalate. Weak enemies can become dangerous. Peace requires maintenance.

Every arc must include a problem introduced early, signs of escalation, consequence if left unresolved.

> Ylila is not attacked suddenly — it is pressured slowly.

## Healer Identity Rule (Orange)

Healing is not just recovery. It is restoration, stabilization, enhancement through consistency.

> Healing reveals what is broken… not just physically.

Orange edge: improve long-term condition (not instant power), create subtle permanent changes, strengthen systems (body, land, creatures).

Weak in burst combat. Strong in sustained situations.

> Orange does not win fights directly. He makes winning possible.

## Slime Narrative Function

Slimes reflect the world around them.

> Slimes become what they are exposed to.

Neglected area → unstable slimes. Nurtured area → cooperative slimes. Corrupted area → aggressive evolution.

Story use: early warning system, environmental indicator, emotional mirror.

> Slimes are not just monsters — they are feedback.

## Volume 1 Progression Path

Act 1 Foundation: village life, routines, relationships, small irregularities.
Act 2 Disruption: minor problems increase, serious training, first combat struggles, healing limits exposed.
Act 3 Pressure: coordinated threats, strained resources, worry, deeper healing potential.
Act 4 Defense of Ylila: organized crisis, teamwork required, no one strong enough alone.
Act 5 Resolution: Ylila survives but changed, systems improve, Orange recognized as essential not strongest.
