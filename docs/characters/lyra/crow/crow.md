# Crow — Slime Partner of Lyra (Locked)

![Crow full body](crow-solo-v1.jpg)

Partner of [Lyra Arroway](../lyra-arroway.md). Silent, observant, perception-focused. Color: black (locked).

## SLIME DESIGN RULE (LOCKED)

Iconic slime form: classic droplet blob. May stretch semi-limb extensions, never humanoid in shape — no legs, no standing body.

## Evolution Path

Stage 1 Scope Form: becomes eye-piece / lens, enhances vision clarity. Magnification, precise aiming.

Stage 2 Spectrum Sight: visual layers — heat / infrared, movement patterns, hidden presence.

> Nothing escapes her sight.

Stage 3 Cloak Form: adaptive cloak / armor, blends with environment. Camouflage, reduced presence, silent movement.

> Before you reach her… you must find her.

Stage 4 Shadow Slime: partially separates, operates independently in shadows. Forward scouting, silent tracking, information relay.

SAS v1.1: rests on Lyra's back as second eyes outside combat. Passive watch for subtle movement. Rarely surprised, never immune. Combat: lens, enhanced vision, cloak support. Color stays black, not grey.

## Limitation (Shared Slime Rule)

Cannot create energy. Only stores, redistributes, enhances efficiency. Extends sustainability, not power.

## STAGE 5: Execution Crow (FINAL UPGRADE, LOCKED)

Evolution of Shadow Crow. Full detail also in [Lyra's lock](../lyra-arroway.md).

### CORE IDENTITY

Crow does not just observe— it can act on what it learns.

FORM: Execution Crow (Evolution of Shadow Crow — Stage 5)

### ACTIVATION FLOW (VERY IMPORTANT)

Execution Crow can ONLY activate after:

1. ✔ Shadow Crow scouting
2. ✔ target observation
3. ✔ pattern confirmation
4. ✔ vulnerability identified

❗ Core Rule: No information → No execution.

### FUNCTION

Shadow Persistence: Crow remains independent from Lyra, operates within shadow space near the target. Lyra stays at range, no reposition required.

Execution Trigger: Crow transforms into a shadow assassin form. Extremely fast, minimal presence, target-locked movement.

Assassination Strike (Mode 1): one precise lethal attack on a weak point. Best for fragile, exposed enemies.

Rend Strike (Mode 2): multiple rapid strikes on the same weak point. Best for armored enemies and tanks. Breaks armor, opens follow-ups for Lyra.

> “If one strike isn't enough… then strike until it breaks.”

Shadow Reversion: immediately returns to shadow state, resumes scouting form, continues information relay.

### LIMITATIONS (CRITICAL FOR BALANCE)

1. One Strike Only — cannot chain attacks, no sustained combat.
2. Requires Full Setup — cannot activate without prior observation, fails if target pattern is incomplete.
3. Target Restriction — effective on isolated targets, distracted enemies, identified weak points. Weak vs heavily armored targets, high-awareness enemies, unpredictable movement.
4. Failure Risk — target becomes alerted, Crow forced into retreat state.
5. Energy Consumption — high drain, limits repeated use.

### DESIGN IDENTITY (LOCK THIS)

❗ This is NOT Lyra entering melee. ✔ This is information turning into elimination.

### SYSTEM SYNERGY

Within Lyra's kit: Spectrum Sight identifies, Cloak positions, Shadow Crow tracks, Execution Crow eliminates.

With team: Leo holds attention, Amy creates pressure, Orange amplifies timing. The team creates opportunity— Crow executes it.

### BALANCE RULE (VERY IMPORTANT)

❗ If used without preparation → it fails. ✔ If used correctly → it removes a key piece.

### FINAL STATEMENT

Lyra does not approach the enemy— her shadow does.
