# Octo — Slime Partner of Amy (FINAL LOCK)

![Octo full body](octo-solo-v1.jpg)

Partner of [Amethyst "Amy" Ironheart](../amy-ironheart.md). Expressive, adaptive, responds to Amy's intent. Color: pink (locked).

## SLIME DESIGN RULE (LOCKED)

Iconic slime form: classic droplet blob. May stretch semi-limb extensions (blade shapes included), never humanoid in shape — no legs, no standing body. EXEMPTION (SPECIAL CASE): Amy's Stage 3 Partner Dance may form a semi-humanoid partner. Octo only.

## Evolution Path (FINAL ORDER — LOCKED)

### STAGE 1 — Blade Extension (Entry Point)

Slime becomes an extension of Amy's weapon. Extends blade reach, adds follow-through strikes, creates curved slashing arcs beyond normal range.

Combat use: safer engagement distance, unexpected hit angles, combo continuation.

> “My sword… but longer… and alive.”

Always attached to Amy, easy to use, low cost. First sign of synergy.

### STAGE 2 — Kraken Flow (Body Extension)

Multiple limb extensions while still attached. Generates multiple slime-blade limbs, enables multi-directional attacks, increases pressure and crowd control.

Combat use: spin attacks, area denial, multi-target harassment.

Identity: controlled chaos— but still centered on Amy. Builds directly from blade extension, still body-bound, reinforces her control and rhythm.

### STAGE 3 — Partner Dance (Identity Core)

Slime becomes a separate combat partner — a semi-humanoid partner (exempted special case) wielding slime-formed blades that fights alongside Amy in sync. Anticipates her rhythm, complements without copying, creates combo chains.

Combat feel: Amy strikes → partner follows. Amy moves → partner covers. Amy commits → partner punishes.

This is where she becomes a true Sword Dancer. Defines her fighting style, turns her from fast fighter into duelist with presence.

### STAGE 4 — Unleash the Kraken (Ultimate)

Slime becomes a battle entity: transforms into a large, monstrous blob form, acts semi-independently, overwhelms battlefield zones. Attacks enemies directly, creates chaos pressure, forces openings for Amy.

Identity: not elegance anymore— domination through presence.

BALANCE (LOCKED CHANGE): does NOT exhaust Amy — exhausts the slime instead. After effect: slime becomes weak and dormant, requires recovery time, accelerated by Orange's high-tier food. Creates team dependency, strategic timing, synergy with Orange's system.

### Final Evolution Flow

1. Blade Extension → weapon synergy
2. Kraken Flow → body pressure
3. Partner Dance → identity peak
4. Unleash Kraken → battlefield control

## Limitation (Shared Slime Rule)

Cannot create energy. Only stores, redistributes, enhances efficiency. Extends sustainability, not power.
