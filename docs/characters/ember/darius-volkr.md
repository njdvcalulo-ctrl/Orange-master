# Darius Volkr — The Engineer General (Locked)

Enables Ember's military strength. Practical, not evil — the gray general.

- Age: mid 40s. Race: human.
- Class: Engineer / Artificer.
- Personality: practical, blunt, results-driven. Hidden: secretly respects
  strength earned through effort. Habit: constantly building or fixing.
- Belief: "If the body fails — build something better." Goal: tools that
  make humans stronger than monsters.
- Relationships: needs Rizla's materials, tolerates him; clashes with
  Valen (logic vs theory); respected by frontline soldiers.
- Abilities: weapon crafting, defensive gear, trap systems. Fights with
  tools, gadgets, enhancements. Analyzes enemy weaknesses fast and builds
  counters over time.
- Weaknesses: slow, needs preparation, equipment-dependent.
- Fate (locked): surrenders and helps keep order — he respects the food
  leverage and the effort behind it.
- Voice: "If it breaks, I fix it. If it doesn't exist, I build it."
  "Magic is fine. Steel is reliable."
  "You don't win with ideals. You win with tools."
