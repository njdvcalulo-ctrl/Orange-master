# Rizla Viremont — The Merchant Druid (Locked)

First major antagonist influence. Scams Ylila subtly. Introduces smart danger.

- Age: late 30s. Race: human with nature affinity.
- Class: Druid → High Druid, Merchant Path hybrid.
- Personality: calm, persuasive, always composed. Hidden: calculating
  and opportunistic. Habit: touches plants while thinking; keeps rare herbs.
- Belief: "Who controls supply… controls survival." Goal: control the
  flow of resources, not just wealth.
- Relationships: respects Valen (knowledge), uses Darius (production),
  trusts neither fully. Appears fair to citizens; manipulates pricing subtly.
- Abilities: plant manipulation (vines, roots), growth acceleration,
  terrain control. Indirect combat — controls the battlefield rather than
  engaging. Merchant sense: reads the value of items and people, predicts
  trade outcomes.
- Weaknesses: weak in direct combat, needs preparation and environment,
  overconfident in negotiations.
- Voice: "Fair trade… depends on who understands the value."
  "You brought crops. I offer opportunity."
  "You don't need more — you need better terms."
