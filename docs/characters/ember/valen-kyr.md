Future threat to Orange specifically. Represents system vs anomaly.

- Age: unknown (appears early 40s). Race: human.
- Class: Archmage.
- Personality: cold, precise, analytical. Hidden: obsessed with control
  over chaos. Habit: speaks in exact terms, rarely uses contractions.
- Belief: "Uncontrolled power is a threat. All power must be governed."
  Goal: a perfect mana control system.
- Relationships: sees Rizla as useful but short-sighted; values Darius's
  practicality; sees most people as unrefined.
- Abilities: high-tier casting, barriers, mana suppression. Specialty:
  interrupts casting mid-spell. Senses unstable mana users — he will
  notice Orange eventually.
- Weaknesses: physically weak, over-relies on control systems, struggles
  against unpredictable opponents.
- Voice: "Your control is inefficient."
  "Mana is not emotion. It is structure."
  "Power without discipline is failure."
