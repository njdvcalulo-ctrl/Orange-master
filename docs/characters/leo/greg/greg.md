# Greg — Slime Partner of Leo (Locked)

![Greg full body](greg-solo-v1.jpg)

Partner of [Leonard Leo Wardensmith](../leo-wardensmith.md). Protective, adaptive, synergizes with armor. Color: steel-blue (provisional — confirm).

## Evolution Path

Stage 1 Armor Sync: stays under armor, absorbs impact, stabilizes Leo's body. Reduced damage, improved balance.

Stage 2 Full Engulf: expands and engulfs Leo, increases size (teen → adult scale). Higher defense, stronger impact resistance.

Stage 3 Engine Mode: integrates with armor into mechanized system, enhances movement + power. Stronger jumps, impact punches, energy-assisted movement. Shield integrates into forearm: wide = defense, dense = offense.

Stage 4 Juggernaut Mode: massive armored form (~3m+), high energy consumption. Overwhelming presence, ground-and-pound. Last resort, not standard — extreme drain, limited duration, post-use vulnerability requiring Orange + Nochi recovery.

> When this activates… run.

## Limitation (Shared Slime Rule)

Cannot create energy. Only stores, redistributes, enhances efficiency. Extends sustainability, not power. Volume 1 gate: wall now, Engine / Juggernaut late.
