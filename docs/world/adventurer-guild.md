# ADVENTURER GUILD SYSTEM (LOCKED)

The Guild is the central institution in major towns and cities: quest hub, tavern, lodging, diner, trade center. Ylila holds only a small outpost — quest board plus a sometimes-manned appraisal desk, mostly pest control, herb gathering, delivery tasks. Full services live in Ember and beyond.

## Ranks

F → E → D → C → B → A → S, judged on skill, experience, reliability. F means novice, near-normal people with little combat skill. S means hero-level individuals. Higher rank opens higher quests, better pay and loot value, more quests per month, greater reputation.

Team Orange starts at F. First quests are small hunts and basic gathering — Horned Bunnies included.

## Quests

Issued by villagers, merchants, nobles, guild authorities. Types: hunting, gathering (plants, herbs, materials), escort, investigation, subjugation.

Completion needs physical proof: a Horned Bunny hunt submits horns. No proof, no pay.

## Loot

Taken from monsters, plants, environment. Three outlets: submit to the Guild (confirms completion, grants rewards), sell to specialists for best value (blacksmiths for weapons and armor, tailors for cloaks, artisans for tools and enchanted goods, meat shops moving monster meat to restaurants), or sell direct at the Guild for less money but speed.

## Appraisal

Status windows are personal-only. Viewing another person's stats needs a guild-issued device or a rare advanced skill.

## Story use

The guild gives Ylila a working economy, a clear progression ladder, and a natural door for enemies, locations, and conflicts. Early: F-rank pest and herb work. Later: stronger monsters, political quests, war involvement, territory control.

## Expanded reference (late-world only)

- Extra quest types: exploration, special requests. Issued alongside hunting, gathering, escort, investigation, subjugation.
- Extra loot outlet: alchemists for potions, alongside blacksmiths, tailors, artisans, meat shops, and fast guild buyback.
- Party system: adventurers may form named registered parties for better efficiency, role specialization, and shared rewards. No instant high-rank promotion, no skipping requirements, no reward without proof. Rank never equals absolute power.
