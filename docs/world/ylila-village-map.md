# YLILA VILLAGE — MAP SYSTEM (FINAL LOCK, REFINED)

![Labeled map of Ylila](../images/ylila-map-labeled-v2.jpg)

**Type:** Early World Zone / Growth Environment
**Theme:** Balance of Life, Work, and Hidden Danger

---

## CORE CONCEPT

Ylila is not just a village. It is a training ground disguised as ordinary life.

✔ harsh land
✔ limited resources
✔ hidden threats beyond its borders

> “It looks peaceful…
> until you step too far.”

---

## SPATIAL OVERVIEW (IMPORTANT)

Orientation:

* North / Northeast → Forest / Gathering Zone
* East → Lake Merma
* West → Crafting Zone / Plains
* South → Road to Ember
* Center → Village Core

Flow Design: Center → Work → Edge → Danger.

Natural progression: Safety → Responsibility → Risk → Survival.

---

## 1. FARMING ZONE (PRIMARY LIFE RING)

Location: rings most of the village (except the eastern lake side), worked from lanes running west and south.

Description: difficult soil, requires constant effort, improved through John Oathkeeper's mastery.

Key Detail: Oathkeeper residence → west of village center, toward the farm belt and western fields. Farmer's storage area + barns + Ben's house → also west, a bit north of the Oathkeeper residence.

Function: food production, stamina building, discipline training.

System Effect: builds endurance, reinforces repetition mindset.

---

## 2. RESIDENTIAL ZONE (INNER RING)

Location: surrounds the village center.

Description: clustered homes, family-centered living.

Function: emotional grounding, identity formation.

Key Homes (placements locked):

* Orange's Home → recovery, cooking (Marie). Near the farm, southeast storage close by.
* Amy's Home → blade + food preparation. East lakeside on Lake Merma, by Steve's dock.
* Leo's Home → crafting influence. Near center, by the southwest smithy.
* Lyra's Home → stealth + bow mastery. North border treehouse + outpost; Joseph (apprentice) two houses east.

---

## 3. VILLAGE CENTER (CORE HUB)

Description: great sacred mango tree at the middle, open gathering space. The Great Mango (locked): ~300 years old, still fruiting; trunk ~1.5 m thick, height ~20-30 m, canopy ~30-38 m wide; no stone around it — short grass only, spreading exactly as wide as the leaf canopy reaches. Founding shade: John and Edward rested under it and decided to build community around its shade. Ylila hymn honors this shade.

Structures:

✔ Chief Edward's residence (Tank-Bard)
✔ Small Adventurer's Guild
✔ Teresa's Inn (2 rooms, near the guild hut, Team Mango-Tango base)
✔ Village Well (east side)
✔ Marie's Food Stall (southeast)
✔ Nathaly's market stall (southwest of the mango)
✔ Tool Shops (north side)
✔ Small School (center-west inner ring, main building)

Streets (locked): walkway ring at the canopy edge, then 4 main roads — north, east, south, west. South road widest (trade plus danger). Ring roads circle the center: 2 rings fit all 50 families plus public buildings; a 3rd ring may open if space runs out. Both rings are lined with villager houses (bahay-kubo style unless noted, some nipa some metal roofs); named homes sit near their work zones. Lanes connect rows to zones and run out to the farm belt, lake, forest, and forge.

Housing roster (locked): 12 named + 38 numbered = 50. Inner ring: chief house, guild, inn, well, school, stalls, plus 2 inner-lane households — I-1 (well keeper), I-2 (guild hand). West outer (12): Oathkeeper residence, Sopia house on the west border, barns + Ben north of Oathkeeper, W-1 to W-10 along the west lane. Southwest outer (5): Shieldsmith house + smithy, SW-1 to SW-4. North outer (7): Arroway treehouse + outpost, Joseph + one neighbor house east, N-1 to N-5. East outer (8): Ironheart house by the dock, E-1 to E-7 along the shore lane. South outer (8): Bunny hut on the south border, S-1 to S-7 along the south road. Southeast outer (3): SE-1 to SE-3 near Marie's stall and the road. Random villagers are farmers, fishermen, bystanders — no names needed unless a scene adopts one.

Slot order, clockwise from north (locked): treehouse/outpost, Joseph, Joseph neighbor, N-1 to N-5; E-1 to E-3, Ironheart house, E-4 to E-7; Ruel, SE-1 to SE-3; Bunny, S-1 to S-7; Shieldsmith house + smithy, SW-1 to SW-4; barns + Ben, Oathkeeper residence, Sopia house, W-1 to W-10 southward.

Outlying placements (locked): Sopia's house on the west border. Oathkeeper residence west. Storage barns + Ben's house west, a bit north of the Oathkeeper residence. Bunny south border. Elder Ruel southeast (inner edge).

Function: coordination, community interaction, decision-making hub.

This is where plans are made— not where battles are fought.

## 4. TRAINING CLEARING

Location: northwest side. Managed by Chief Edward.

Description: open dirt field, hand-drawn sparring circle.

Equipment: wooden short swords + shields (5 sets), bows with padded arrows (2 sets), crafted by Arroway family.

Function: combat fundamentals, team coordination, controlled conflict.

Rule: "Same rules?" — hit = point, stay inside, anything goes.

---

## 5. GATHERING ZONE (OUTER RING — NORTH/NORTHEAST)

Description: forest edge, herbs, berries, materials, occasional low-tier monsters.

Key Feature: Arroway Treehouse Outpost.

Function: stealth training, terrain mastery, awareness development.

Key Users: Lyra, Silk Arroway.

---

## 6. CRAFTING ZONE

Location: Southwest → Blacksmith (Ethan's smithy, Shieldsmith family). North → Carpentry + Tailoring (Arroway family).

Description: workshops and toolmaking areas.

Function: gear creation, experimentation, optimization mindset.

Key Users: Leo (engineering mindset), Ethan (blacksmith), Nathaly (design).

---

## 7. WATER ZONE

Location: Blueberry River flows from north forest → Lake Merma in the east.

Description: fishing area, docking space, recreational use. Steve's east lakeside dock (main deck, doubles as events/feast space), near Amy's home. Naomi dances here at events.

Function: precision training, patience, controlled movement.

Key Users: Steve (father), Amy (knife precision).

---

## 8. BOUNDARY LINE (CRITICAL ZONE)

Description: no walls. Marked by soil shift, silence, tension.

Rule: "Villagers don't cross it."

Function: psychological barrier, first exposure to danger, decision checkpoint.

---

## 9. OUTER WILD ZONE (DANGER AREA)

Description: uncontrolled environment beyond the boundary.

Regions: North Forest → low-tier monsters. Far West Plains → leads to Boulder Fields (Golems). Acts as natural border to other nations.

Contains: slimes, beasts, evolving threats.

Function: real combat, fear response, decision-making under pressure.

---

## 10. ROAD TO EMBER

Location: south of the village. Dirt road, main connection to the outside world. Widest of the four main roads.

Usage: trade (rare surplus), travel to Ember.

Economic Detail: Ylila lacks natural metal resources, relies on trade and recycling.

---

## SYSTEM DESIGN (LOCKED)

Ylila is designed to: build fundamentals first, introduce danger gradually, reward discipline.

CORE RULE:

> ❗ Ylila does NOT create heroes. ✔ It prepares them to become one.

Inside Ylila: safety, structure, growth.

Outside Ylila: danger, unpredictability, survival pressure.

## SURROUNDINGS — REGION MAP (FINAL LOCK)

NORTH — Forest → Mountains: immediately north is Forest Edge, far north the Mountain Range. Natural system: mountains drain water, rivers flow Mountains → Forest → Lake Merma. Creates natural irrigation, fertile farmland, believable ecosystem.

EAST — Lake Merma (~50x Ylila, shown partly — it runs off the map): fishing (Amy's family), water supply, calm emotional scenes. Fed by a river from the far northwest mountains, running through the forest beyond the treehouse. Also a natural boundary and future expansion zone.

SOUTH — Road to Ember: dirt road, grasslands, rolling hills, leads to Ember City. Trade route, danger route, invasion path (VERY IMPORTANT).

WEST — Plains of Danger: open plains, grasslands. Hidden threats: B-Tier Rock Golems, A-Rank Crystal Golem (territorial). Almost impossible to cross safely — a natural wall to the western noble cities. No artificial borders needed; the world blocks itself.

Built-in story structure: North → exploration, East → peace and bonding, South → conflict, West → high danger and late game. Layered progression: village life → clearing → forest → river → deeper zones; road → Ember → war; plains → late-game threat.
