# Ylila Village (Environment)

Small farming village, ~50 families. Harsh plain, difficult farming, naturally low yield — yet sustains itself through discipline.

## Identity

Peaceful but not weak. Simple but not ignorant. Growing but still vulnerable.

> A place where people live properly… not perfectly.

## Livelihood

Farming, fishing, crafting, simple trade. John Oathkeeper's land thrives and supplies high-quality rice — reason Ylila sustains itself.

## Tone

Warm, grounded, growth-focused. Safe haven where strength is rebuilt through meals, rest, routines.

## Fragility (Locked)

Peace requires maintenance. Small monster / ecosystem / outsider problems escalate if ignored. Ylila is pressured slowly, not attacked suddenly.

Volume 1 ends with Ylila becoming a fortified safe haven — surviving but changed.
