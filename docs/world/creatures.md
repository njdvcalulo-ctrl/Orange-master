# CREATURE SYSTEM (LOCKED)

Design rule: not random creatures — an ecosystem with purpose. Each creature feeds the village, creates small problems, and supports future systems. Tools built now, deployed when needed.

## Horned Rabbits

Horns: dull (non-lethal). Behavior: crop eaters (especially kamatis and leafy greens), skittish but stubborn. Use: primary meat source, can be domesticated. Tone: more nuisance than threat — early game perfect.

## Tree Gecko (NEW)

Habitat: trees, forest edge, village outskirts. Traits: excellent climbers, bark-blending camouflage, sticky feet (runs upside down on branches). Behavior: harmless, eats insects (like electric bee larvae). Uses: pest control, possible future scout-type companion. Lyra will 100% notice these first.

## Fish System (Lake Life)

Red Milk Fish: soft nutritious meat, high stamina recovery (natural, not magical). Popular in village meals (Marie).

Checker Tilapia: hardy, easy to farm, common everyday food. Good for fishing scenes, calm village moments, Amy bonding scenes.

## Verdant Hydra (Contained Threat — Locked Rules)

Regeneration: severed heads regrow doubled. Overload slows the body — too many heads and it cannot carry its own growth. Body (locked): thick armored scales, high physical and magical resistance — a legendary-grade creature. Super-regeneration applies to head parts only; a true kill needs a strike to the body, to the heart. Team Carrot found the loophole and exploits instead of killing.
Farming loop (locked): Xavier cuts heads → heads multiply → excess heads collapse its mobility, which makes further cutting and harvesting easier → severed heads fall as loot when cut → team collects teeth from dropped heads only, never from the live beast → team stops when loaded, never greedy → team leaves the area → hydra reverts after days. Reversion (final): it returns to 3 heads with teeth regenerated in base form; teeth are removed again later by Edward's shield-bash or defensive clashes. No infinite abuse: every cycle demands skill and timing, so it feels earned. Outcome (locked): the party never captured the adult — they took an egg and left it behind, reverted and toothless in its cave, uncontained. The egg is the delayed payoff: ally, uncontrollable, or tragedy — undecided.
Containment, not control: it behaves only while weakened, restrained, and mana-influenced — Serra stabilizes, Xavier contains, Elinore positions, Edward calls timing. It takes the whole party. Uncontained, it attacks anything.
Use: break formations, stall a boss, force attention — never the win. It has instinct, not judgment; it distracts the enemy's strongest but cannot defeat them.
Orange hook (future): his Grow magic would accelerate its regeneration and steady its frenzy — and make it more dangerous. Helping is risk; healing is responsibility.
Lair: a Caldora cave that was a golem nest first — broken golem remains inside, mana cores eaten; a stuffed hydra may not feed for a week or two. It lured the party in and blocked the escape. Its soft neck is a lure-weapon: it lets you cut so it gains heads to eat you with. Its armored body is beyond the party's piercing — the heart strike is known but impossible, so they exploit instead.
Temperament: toothless but not harmless. It sulks, snaps, and tries to bite. The team argues about it.

Herbs (healing, crafting). Wood types: rubber trees, coconut trees, hardwood (construction), fruit-bearing trees including mango, banana, papaya. Small non-hostile animals. Great Mango at center is sacred and not for timber.

## More Low-Tier Fauna

Rock Birds: hardened head and back, soft underbelly. Can be domesticated. Poultry meat source. Ylila local chicken for adobo.

Furry Pigs (NEW barn animal): round, soft-haired, docile. Barn-kept by Ben. Pork source for festive adobo. Kid-friendly, not a combat threat.

Lake Frogs: estuarine frogs around Lake Merma. Seasonal catch with Steve and Amy. Frog-adobo option in rainy season.

Electric Bees: fast, aggressive if provoked, mild shock attacks. Good for early danger and teamwork lessons. (Larvae eaten by tree geckos; honey → future stamina drink.)

Wind Dogs: fast, agile, can be tamed, natural sprinters. Future potential: mounts, scouts.
