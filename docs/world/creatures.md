# CREATURE SYSTEM (LOCKED)

Design rule: not random creatures — an ecosystem with purpose. Each creature feeds the village, creates small problems, and supports future systems. Tools built now, deployed when needed.

## Horned Rabbits

Horns: dull (non-lethal). Behavior: crop eaters (especially carrots), skittish but stubborn. Use: primary meat source, can be domesticated. Tone: more nuisance than threat — early game perfect.

## Tree Gecko (NEW)

Habitat: trees, forest edge, village outskirts. Traits: excellent climbers, bark-blending camouflage, sticky feet (runs upside down on branches). Behavior: harmless, eats insects (like electric bee larvae). Uses: pest control, possible future scout-type companion. Lyra will 100% notice these first.

## Fish System (Lake Life)

Red Milk Fish: soft nutritious meat, high stamina recovery (natural, not magical). Popular in village meals (Marie).

Checker Tilapia: hardy, easy to farm, common everyday food. Good for fishing scenes, calm village moments, Amy bonding scenes.

## Future Hooks (NOT NOW)

Electric bee honey → stamina drink. Gecko oil → climbing aid. Rabbit leather → light armor. Tools for later phases.

## Forest Edge Resources

Herbs (healing, crafting). Wood types: rubber trees, coconut trees, hardwood (construction), fruit-bearing trees. Small non-hostile animals.

## More Low-Tier Fauna

Rock Birds: hardened head and back, soft underbelly. Can be domesticated. Poultry meat source.

Electric Bees: fast, aggressive if provoked, mild shock attacks. Good for early danger and teamwork lessons. (Larvae eaten by tree geckos; honey → future stamina drink.)

Wind Dogs: fast, agile, can be tamed, natural sprinters. Future potential: mounts, scouts.
