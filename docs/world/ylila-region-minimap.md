## Inner Village Layout (mental model, no exact measurements)

```
          [Forest]
              |
   [Training Clearing]
              |
 [Farm] — [Village Center] — [Lake]
              |
         [South Road]
```

Key local positions: Village Core holds Edward's office, the small
guild hub, Teresa's inn (2 rooms), and storage barns. Edward's residence
sits northwest (office is center, house is not). Oathkeeper Farm lies
west of center toward the farm belt. Training Clearing sits between the
village and the forest, managed by Edward. Amy's house, docks, and calm
scenes live lakeside east.
