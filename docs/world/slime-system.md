# SLIME SYSTEM (FINAL LOCK)

Semi-intelligent, adaptive mana lifeforms. Slimes grow with you — not for you.

## 1. Core nature

Mana-based, highly adaptive, semi-intelligent at base. Naturally harmless or neutral. React to environment, follow instinct, can attach to objects or beings.

Slimes become what they are exposed to: neglected area → unstable, nurtured area → cooperative, corrupted area → aggressive evolution. They act as early warning, environmental indicator, emotional mirror.

## 2. Variants (current, locked)

- Green → defense / durability ([Greg](../characters/leo/greg/greg.md), Leo's partner)
- Purple → flexibility / weapon-type ([Octo](../characters/amy/octo/octo.md), Amy's partner)
- Black → perception / vision ([Crow](../characters/lyra/crow/crow.md), Lyra's partner)
- Orange → growth / support ([Nochi](../characters/orange/nochi/nochi.md), Orange's partner)
- Blue → King-type / authority (the Slime King only — colorless while corrupted, restored blue upon healing; unique, not a wild variant)

Rules: iconic blob form. Semi-limb stretch is allowed. Never humanoid — except Octo Stage 3 Partner Dance, which is the one locked exemption. Colors are plushie-ready and fixed as above. Grey from older drafts is NOT current. Blue belongs to the restored Slime King alone — colorless while corrupted, blue again upon healing. No wild blue slimes.

## 3. Intelligence and bonding

Slimes do NOT dominate users. They assist, adapt, learn. The user keeps full control; the slime enhances capability. Bond scale (locked): 1 Neutral (wild), 2 Tolerant (won't attack), 3 Friendly (follows), 4 Bonded (SAS assist begins), 5 Synchronized (advanced forms unlock). Bond grows through time together, shared combat, shared survival, and consistent feeding — for Orange's team, his cooking. Result: better response, faster adaptation, more complex behavior. As bond deepens, the slime understands what the user needs. Later stages may communicate and show personality.

Superseded from older Assist drafts: slimes do NOT consume the user's mana as fuel — they run on stored food energy with fatigue and cooldown limits. And Orange does have SAS — the most developed one (Nochi). Both points stay rejected.

## 4. SAS origin (FINAL — food-triggered)

SAS is Symbiotic Adaptation System (FINAL name, not Slime Assist System).

CORRECTION to older drafts: SAS is NOT a Grow-spell accident. SAS is triggered by Orange's cooking carrying a growth signal: consistent eating + bonding + fighting alongside causes adaptation.

Requires intent, care, consistency, relationship. Catchphrase: "…Maybe it's because of the food." Enemies cannot mass-produce or steal it — without the relationship, the food is just food.

Superseded (locked): the older Grow-accident draft — Grow splashing onto clinging slimes creating Slime Assist System — is rejected. Grow enhances efficiency and never mutates slimes by itself. SAS means Symbiotic Adaptation System, food-triggered, not spell side-effect.

Orange's Grow skill enhances efficiency and control. It does not mutate slimes by itself.

## 5. Core functions

Change form, reinforce body or equipment, enhance perception, extend reach, adapt to user needs. While attached, SAS slimes transfer mana at will: sustain Leo's tanking, hold Lyra's focus shots, extend Amy's combos, amplify Orange's support.

Limits (locked): transfer is not infinite. Slimes fatigue and need cooldown. Nochi cannot create energy — only stores, redistributes, enhances efficiency. Multiply splits the store, never copies it. Overuse drains reserves. Needs feeding first.

## 6. Evolution by partner style

- Tank (Leo + Greg): armor coating, impact absorption, corrosion resistance → Engine Mode → Juggernaut (last resort, heavy drain).
- Archer (Lyra + Crow): Scope Form, Spectrum Sight (heat/movement/hidden), Cloak Form, Shadow Crow (independent scout), Execution Crow Stage 5 (one precise strike, full setup required, Lyra stays at range).
- Melee (Amy + Octo): Blade Extension, Kraken Flow, Partner Dance (exemption core), Unleash the Kraken (drains slime, needs Orange's food to recover).
- Support (Orange + Nochi): Storage Core, Enhance, Multiply, Cocoon emergency. Late game: Nature Sync ambient absorption — still storage, cooking stays strongest.

No instant evolution. No overpowering independence. No mass production.

## 7. World perception and hooks

Normal view: slimes are weak creatures. SAS reality: rare, misunderstood, valuable. Future hooks: scholars study them, guilds regulate them, enemies attempt capture — and fail to replicate without the bond.

Design principle: alive, responsive, unique per character.
