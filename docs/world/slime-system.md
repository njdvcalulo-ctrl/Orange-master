# SLIME SYSTEM (FINAL LOCK)

Semi-intelligent, adaptive mana lifeforms. Slimes grow with you — not for you.

## 1. Core nature

Mana-based, highly adaptive, semi-intelligent at base. Naturally harmless or neutral. React to environment, follow instinct, can attach to objects or beings. Body is transparent jelly around a core stone; the core keeps it alive and is its weak point — transparency is vulnerability.

Slimes become what they are exposed to: neglected area → unstable, nurtured area → cooperative, corrupted area → aggressive evolution. They act as early warning, environmental indicator, emotional mirror.

## 2. Tendencies (SAS-driven, locked)

Tendencies come from SAS, not from nature alone. Same color does not guarantee same attacks. At most they grant comfort and movement, never attack spells.

- Green → defense / durability, earth-flavored harden ([Greg](../characters/leo/greg/greg.md), Leo's partner)
- Purple → flexibility / weapon-type, wind-flavored cut ([Octo](../characters/amy/octo/octo.md), Amy's partner)
- Black → perception / vision, shadow-flavored cloak and scout ([Crow](../characters/lyra/crow/crow.md), Lyra's partner). Shadow only. No darkness element, no dark magic.
- Orange → growth / support ([Nochi](../characters/orange/nochi/nochi.md), Orange's partner)
- Blue → water-flavored comfort, water-walker at most, never water attacks. Wild blues exist. Ylila's Slime King happens to be blue.

Other regions rumor other tendencies (for example red warm-spit and lava-walker, never lava spit or fire). Not in Ylila. Subject to change.

Rules: iconic blob form. Semi-limb stretch is allowed. Never humanoid — except Octo Stage 3 Partner Dance, which is the one locked exemption. Touch: bonded and cooperative slimes control composition and are safe to handle; wild slimes still burn. Team colors stay fixed as above: Nochi orange, Greg green, Octo purple, Crow black. Grey from older drafts is NOT current. Colorless means mindless and corrupted to the core — chaos, not a type.

## 3. Intelligence and bonding

Slimes do NOT dominate users. They assist, adapt, learn. The user keeps full control; the slime enhances capability. Bond scale (locked): 1 Neutral (wild), 2 Tolerant (won't attack), 3 Friendly (follows), 4 Bonded (SAS assist begins), 5 Synchronized (advanced forms unlock). Bond grows through time together, shared combat, shared survival, and consistent feeding — for Orange's team, his cooking. Result: better response, faster adaptation, more complex behavior. As bond deepens, the slime understands what the user needs. Later stages may communicate and show personality. Speech caps at toddler level for bonded slimes; the King alone speaks with full intelligence.

Superseded from older Assist drafts: slimes do NOT consume the user's mana as fuel — they run on stored food energy with fatigue and cooldown limits. And Orange does have SAS — the most developed one (Nochi). Both points stay rejected.

## 4. SAS origin (FINAL — food-triggered)

SAS is Symbiotic Adaptation System (FINAL name, not Slime Assist System).

CORRECTION to older drafts: SAS is NOT a Grow-spell accident. SAS is triggered by Orange's cooking carrying a growth signal: consistent eating + bonding + fighting alongside causes adaptation.

Requires intent, care, consistency, relationship. Catchphrase: "…Maybe it's because of the food." Enemies cannot mass-produce or steal it — without the relationship, the food is just food.

Superseded (locked): the older Grow-accident draft — Grow splashing onto clinging slimes creating Slime Assist System — is rejected. Grow enhances efficiency and never mutates slimes by itself. SAS means Symbiotic Adaptation System, food-triggered, not spell side-effect.

Orange's Grow skill enhances efficiency and control. It does not mutate slimes by itself.

## 5. Core functions

Change form, reinforce body or equipment, enhance perception, extend reach, adapt to user needs. While attached, SAS slimes transfer mana at will: sustain Leo's tanking, hold Lyra's focus shots, extend Amy's combos, amplify Orange's support.

Limits (locked): transfer is not infinite. Slimes fatigue and need cooldown. Nochi cannot create energy — only stores, redistributes, enhances efficiency. Multiply splits the store, never copies it. Temporary splits dry out if spent and return to the main blob if mana remains. Overuse drains reserves. Needs feeding first.

King-tier (rare, any color, locked): about 1 in a million plus accidents. Needs all of these: very old slime, fed and nourished colony, abandoned-forest conditions with room to spread, and slime level 50. Overeat bare land like a virus and they dry up and most or all die. Well-nourished land lets them eat less, rest, and hibernate instead of swarming. Most slimes die first as lower food chain. Slimes lack firepower against big monsters, so numbers alone do not win.

King Shell (locked): the limiter. Grown yolk-core at 50 triggers an option to shell up, never auto. The slime hides, finds shelter, and secretes mucus that hardens outside while keeping moisture inside — like an egg: jelly-white outside, growing yolk-core inside. Fully vulnerable while shelled. Disturbed shell fails. Distinct from Nochi Cocoon emergency ally-heal. Team Orange slimes are an SAS anomaly and level fast by experience, which is a future problem for enemies — but bonded kingship still needs King Shell plus weaken, so no accidental kings in Volumes 1–3.

## 6. Evolution by partner style

- Tank (Leo + Greg): armor coating, impact absorption, corrosion resistance → Engine Mode → Juggernaut (last resort, heavy drain).
- Archer (Lyra + Crow): Scope Form, Spectrum Sight (heat/movement/hidden), Cloak Form, Shadow Crow (independent scout), Execution Crow Stage 5 (one precise strike, full setup required, Lyra stays at range).
- Melee (Amy + Octo): Blade Extension, Kraken Flow, Partner Dance (exemption core), Unleash the Kraken (drains slime, needs Orange's food to recover).
- Support (Orange + Nochi): Storage Core, Enhance, Multiply, Cocoon emergency. Late game: Nature Sync ambient absorption — still storage, cooking stays strongest.

No instant evolution. No overpowering independence. No mass production.

## 7. World perception and hooks

Normal view: slimes are weak creatures. SAS reality: rare, misunderstood, valuable. Future hooks: scholars study them, guilds regulate them, enemies attempt capture — and fail to replicate without the bond.

Design principle: alive, responsive, unique per character.
