# STAT SYSTEM (FINAL — v2.1 + v2.2 LOCKED)

Core definitions (v2.1): STR physical power, melee damage, equipment requirements, carrying capacity. INT magic power, mana efficiency, MP capacity, magical defense. AGI movement speed, attack speed, physical execution speed — combo speed, dodge capability, repositioning. DEX casting speed, accuracy, control. VIT HP, stamina, physical defense. Luck/Blessing crits, dodge, favor and charisma.

Equipment and weight: every item carries a STR requirement (heavy armor ~30, light clothing ~2). Overburden past STR capacity drains stamina faster, slows movement, drops combat efficiency. No artificial balance needed — STR gates weight, AGI gates speed, INT gates mana, VIT gates survival.

Stamina web (v2.2, confirmed): VIT sets pool and recovery, STR handles load, AGI governs movement efficiency. STR plus VIT plus AGI form one connected engine. This is why farm labor and forge work build real combat endurance.

## Stamina interaction (confirmed)

- VIT sets stamina pool and recovery. Low VIT drains fast.
- STR handles load. Hauling beyond your STR burns extra stamina.
- AGI governs movement efficiency. High AGI wastes less on the move.

STR plus VIT plus AGI form one connected engine. This is why farm labor and forge work build real combat endurance.

## Luck / Blessing (expanded)

Three functions:

1. Combat: critical hit chance, dodge chance.
2. Social: charisma weight, first impressions, negotiation outcomes, trade prices, contract success. The merchant's, leader's, and party face's stat.
3. Presence: attractiveness, natural charm, aura — features, appeal, even fashion sense.

Inheritance: partially heritable. Appealing parents raise the base. Growth cap (locked): natural base plus 20 maximum. Nobody grinds into a charisma god. Identity stays grounded.

Tone rule: keep it light and in-world. Never "you're ugly." Use "not exactly the face of the party," "you hit hard — let someone else do the talking," that register.

## Status window and leveling

Say "Status" to open a personal window only the user sees: name, age, race, class/job, level, EXP current/required, titles, status condition, available stat points, skills with levels.

Range 0–1000. Scale: 50 average adult, 70 skilled professional, 100+ elite, 200 king-level, 800 dragon-tier, 900 angel/demon-tier, 1000 peak beings. Team Orange starts at the bottom of this ladder — Volume 1 is single digits climbing slowly.

Each level: +3 allocable points, +2 auto-allocated by usage (fighting → STR/AGI, casting → INT/DEX), +1 skill upgrade point. Skills feed stats back (Swordsmanship Lv.1 +1 STR, Lv.2 +1 AGI; higher tiers grant more specialized bonuses). Fractional gains hide until whole numbers — +1 shows, +0.1 does not — the same visibility rule behind Orange's +0.05% buff growth.

Stats grow through leveling, training, and skill development only. In story, show the window sparingly — let actions reflect stats naturally.

Balance is player-driven, never forced penalties. High STR never auto-slows casting; points are simply finite, so strength somewhere means neglect elsewhere. Builds: melee STR + AGI, archer AGI + DEX, mage/support INT + DEX, tank VIT + INT (VIT physical defense, INT magical defense). Max-STR-only makes a slow glass-cannon bruiser; high AGI with low VIT is fast but fragile; high INT with low VIT hits hard and breaks easy. Freedom of build, responsibility of consequence. No instant max stats, no skipped spikes.

## Principle

Not all power shows in battle. Some wins happen before the fight begins. The stat system is now mechanically complete, naturally balanced, story-compatible, character-driven.
