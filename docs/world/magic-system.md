# MAGIC AND CASTING (FINAL LOCK)

Physical skills use stamina. Magic skills use mana. Fighters tire, mages run dry — Orange is the exception readers notice, and the story never explains it early.

## Core logic

Mana exists everywhere and surrounds all beings. Magic is sensing mana, controlling it, shaping it into effects. Not everyone can control mana — some never learn it, some train for years, a few have natural talent.

Two user types: standard users (majority) learn through study, training, repetition — mages, healers, elemental users. Innate users (rare) are born with natural control. Orange belongs here: instinctive, evolving, unique control — which sets his starting point, never his finish. Every gain still costs practice.

## Cast initiation

Skills activate by speaking the skill name — the same calling the kids practice in afternoon training ("Strike", "Guard", "Pierce"). To begin: enough mana (or stamina for physical), proper focus, able to act (not stunned or silenced).

Casting is not instant unless specified. While casting the user focuses, gathers mana, shapes the skill. Movement may narrow and the caster sits in a vulnerability window.

## Interruption

Casting breaks on damage, lost focus, or disruption (knockback, stun, fear). A broken cast fails, eats part of the mana, and may still trigger partial or full cooldown by skill.

Resistance comes from INT (mental stability) and DEX (control precision). High INT plus DEX holds casts together under pressure.

## Instant casts and cooldowns

Some skills fire instantly. Trade-off: real cooldowns, no spam. Every skill cools down after use. Instant and strong skills carry longer cooldowns. Mastery and relevant stats shorten them — STR-side for physical, DEX/INT-side for magic. Example scale: an early instant like Speed Boost sits around 10–30 seconds. Physical parallel: Bash cooldowns shrink with Strength.

Magic improves through repeated use, experience, mastery: shorter casts, shorter cooldowns, stronger effect. Skills refine along two tracks — linear tiers through mastery (Heal → Greater Heal → Restoration) and identity evolution through style (HOW you train shapes WHAT it becomes, e.g. sword work danced well becomes Sword Dancer). Tiers without discipline evolve nothing.

## Combos and timing

Chain for effect: sequential (Freeze → Slash → Shatter), enhancement (buff → attack, e.g. Speed Boost → Dash Strike), team (Leo holds → Orange buffs → Lyra or Amy finishes). Combat rewards timing, positioning, coordination. Fast is not automatically successful — bad timing wastes the skill.

## Multi-casting (advanced, gated)

Overlapping skills or limited cast-while-moving needs high DEX plus high INT. Rare, never for beginners. Nobody in Team Orange touches this in Volume 1.

## Mastery

Higher skill level: shorter cast time, lower cost, better control, harder to interrupt.

## New skill acquisition

Learning a skill pops a floating hologram like Status: skill name, type, basic usage. It can arrive mid-combat — quick read, instinctive first try. Built for clutch moments, not lectures.

## Cast time logic

Stronger magic means longer cast, reduced by skill mastery plus DEX. Mastery is familiarity, DEX is mana control precision. Beginners cast slow and exposed. Experts cast fast, efficient, near-instant.

Cast types: Instant (no cast time, cooldown-based), Short (quick), Standard (normal), Fast (mastery plus DEX reduced), Long (high-tier), Channeling (continuous drain).

## Stat influence (final)

- STR: physical skill power and technique efficiency.
- AGI: movement speed and physical execution speed.
- DEX: casting speed, accuracy, skill control, mana manipulation precision.
- INT: magic power, mana efficiency, mental stability under pressure — resists panic, confusion, mental fatigue.

Conceptual summary: cast speed from mastery plus DEX, spell power from INT (magic) or STR (physical), combat performance from AGI plus VIT plus INT control. Mastery and control define power, not raw stats alone.

## Balance

No skill is always safe, always instant, always guaranteed. Every cast carries risk, timing, and consequence. Casting is a skill, not a shortcut.

## Orange's magic (special system, locked)

Innate affinity, trained like everyone else. Orange learns through instinct, repetition, experience — affinity never skips practice, control training, or mana limits. No silent casting until mastered much later. No instant high-tier magic. Gradual growth only.

Nature: initially colorless, evolving into a faint orange sunset tone. Sensation warm and calming, slightly addictive — comfort, not compulsion.

Early-stage tree (values scale with mastery):

1. Regeneration (passive): constant self-healing. Resting about 5% HP/stamina per hour, active about 1%. Improves with control — this is why he out-repeats everyone.
2. Healing: self first, then others.
3. Cure: removes poison, toxins, abnormal conditions.
4. Buff (Status Up): about +20% effective output for about 5 seconds early. Read as efficiency and stability expressing as output, not raw power inflation — consistent with Grow removing waste. Hidden effect: +0.05% permanent stat growth per application, visible only at whole numbers. Slow visible growth by design.
5. Grow (advanced buff): enhances the target's specialization by making it cleaner — fire mage's fire travels truer, archer's aim holds steadier. High mana cost. Small damage gains arrive through efficiency, never as direct power conjured from nothing.
6. Anti-undead: healing harms undead and heals the living. Reverse interaction, same spell.

## Channeling rule

Some skills need continuous casting. Behavior depends on the skill type, not one fixed rule.

### 1. Stationary channeling

Examples: Defensive Wall, Healing Field, Energy Beam.

- Drains mana continuously.
- Stops on interrupt (damage, knockback), excess movement, or empty mana.
- Built for defensive control, area support, sustained output.

Volume 1 use: short, fragile, team-protected. Orange's Healing Field only works if Leo holds and Lyra watches.

### 2. Mobile channeling (stealth-type)

Example: Stealth, including Lyra's Cloak Form and Veil Step with Crow.

- Movement alone does NOT break it.
- Breaks on detection (enemy scan or reveal skills, devices), empty mana, or clumsy contact — colliding with terrain, bumping people, disturbing the environment unnaturally.

Environment matters: calm places expose small disturbances, chaos hides them. Silent hallway hides nothing; a loud battlefield hides movement.

Detection depends on enemy DEX (perception and precision) plus detection skills. High DEX enemies sense presence and track subtle motion.

## Channeling balance

Not all channeling roots the user. Not all movement is safe. Each skill defines its own limits and break conditions. Channeling is sustained control, adapted to the skill — which keeps stealth scenes smart, detection fair, and positioning tactical.
