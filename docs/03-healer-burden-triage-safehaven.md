# Healer Burden, Triage, Safe Haven (Locked)

## Healer Burden System

> The more lives Orange tries to carry, the more strain he accumulates.

Types:

1. Physical Fatigue — body weakens, slower reaction, reduced stamina.
2. Mental Load — decision pressure, prioritization stress, mistakes more likely.
3. Emotional Strain — grief accumulation, compassion fatigue, detachment risk.

> Not everyone can be saved.

Orange must choose who to help first. Saving one may risk another. Failure is part of growth.

> Healing is not victory — it is responsibility.

## Triage System (Combat + Healing)

> Priority is not fairness.

Orange decides who is most at risk, who can still be saved, who must be let go.

Creates tension, forces decisions, challenges identity. Guilt, doubt, questioning purpose follow.

> A healer's hardest skill is not healing — it is choosing.

## Safe Haven Rule (Ylila)

Functions: physical recovery, emotional grounding, relational support. Elements: family warmth, shared meals, simple routines, unconditional care.

> Strength is rebuilt here.

After major strain, Orange returns to reset. Relationships deepen. Lessons processed.

> Without Ylila, Orange will eventually break.
