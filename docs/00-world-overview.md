# World Overview — Ylila Village Arc

Title: **Orange: The Healer's Resolve**
Scope: Early World, Volume 1 focus — Ylila Village.

## World

Grounded medieval fantasy world with:

- swords, shields, bows
- magic (mana-based, but not overwhelming)
- monsters (slimes, horned rabbits, goblins, etc.)
- evolving systems (skills, growth, professions)

> This is NOT a high-fantasy power world yet. No overpowered heroes. No instant mastery. Growth is earned, slow, and consistent.

## Ylila Village

- Small farming village, ~50 families.
- Built through hard work, not magic.
- Focus: farming, fishing, crafting, simple trade.

Tone of village: peaceful but not weak. Simple but not ignorant. Growing but still vulnerable.

Key identity: "A place where people live properly… not perfectly."

## Story Tone

- Overall: grounded, warm, growth-focused.
- Emotional core: family, friendship, discipline, becoming better slowly.

## Faith (Subtle, Locked)

A Creator exists: eternal, personal, holy, source of life, mana, and order. Essence is translated into fantasy, never copied from real-world theology, never lectured.

Soul rule: humans are bearers of the divine image with full spiritual capacity. Other races are fragments of creation — will, intelligence, able to respond to grace, but not image-bearers in full.

Orange is a bridge, not a preacher: he loves monsters, heals them, forms alliances, and draws them closer to the Creator. Faith shows through care, never sermons.

## Hard Restrictions

- No sudden overpowered abilities.
- No breaking system rules.
- No ignoring established mechanics.
- No replacing effort with luck.

## AI Behavior In This World

When generating scenes:

- show growth, don't explain it
- keep abilities consistent
- respect limitations
- prioritize character development

## Goal of Volume 1

- Establish Ylila as a living village.
- Show early growth of the team.
- Build relationships and systems.
- End with Ylila becoming a fortified safe haven.

## Final Core Statement

> This is a story where strength is built, not given… and where even the strongest still need others.
