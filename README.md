# Orange: The Healer's Resolve — Story Bible

![Cover — Team Orange, Volume 1 Ylila](docs/images/cover-team-v1.jpg)

A grounded medieval-fantasy light novel about Orange Oathkeeper, a young healer who can fight and kill but wins by making sure his team can. Set in Ylila, a small radial farming village of ~150 people, Volume 1 follows Orange, his slime partner Nochi, and their friends as slow, earned growth turns a peaceful village into a fortified safe haven. Volume 2 ends with the fall of Ember and the rise of Carrot Castle. Volume 3 is proposed as fields first.

Scope: Early World, Volumes 1–2 complete in draft, Volume 3 Fields First proposed.

> A place where people live properly… not perfectly.

> This is a story where strength is built, not given… and where even the strongest still need others.

## Locked Core Rules

- Food ≠ Power. Process = Power.
- Power comes from process, not talent alone.
- Skills are learned, not given.
- How you do the work shapes what the skill becomes (Skill Evolution).
- Growth is effort-based. No random spikes. No chosen one.
- Orange can fight and kill but is not the damage dealer. He wins by making sure his team can.
- Healing is not victory — it is responsibility.
- Priority is not fairness.
- Without Ylila, Orange will eventually break.
- Orange's cooking carries a growth signal (SAS = Symbiotic Adaptation System) — power that must be shared, never stolen. The older Grow-accident origin is rejected. Grey slimes are not current; Ylila's Slime King happens to be blue after restoration, but kingship is any-color rare tier. SAS slimes stay bonded partners, never tamed summons.
- Volume 1 caps: base classes only, short fragile channels, single-digit stats climbing slowly. Ylila registers base classes only.
- Leo starts Fighter and earns Warden; Warden is never a base class.
- Orange has affinity but still requires practice. His hybrid name stays unassigned and distinct from Serra.
- Early game: everyone starts simple before specializing.
- Balance is player-driven through finite points, never forced penalties: high STR never auto-slows casting, high DEX never auto-cuts durability.
- Every cast carries risk, timing, and consequence: no silent casting until mastered later, no instant high-tier magic.
- Orange's Buffs carry hidden +0.05% permanent growth visible at whole numbers; gains read as efficiency, never conjured power.
- Grow makes training cleaner so skills master faster and can evolve sooner; temporary window, high cost.
- Orange grows gradually within mana, stamina, and fatigue limits; late power stays late, hard-earned, and never Volume 1.
- Status windows are personal-only; slimes run on stored food energy with fatigue and cooldowns, transfer stays finite.
- The Guild rewards consistency: rank never equals power, no reward without proof, no instant promotion.
- Faith stays subtle early and translated, never forced; Orange is a bridge who heals and befriends. House of Restoration guidance grows later through Serra, by care first, words second.
- Dreams are rare symbolic guidance, sometimes only presence; guidance never replaces training.
- Orange's body path is gated: farm boy endurance first, self-healing tank mid, superhuman late, vessel endgame only.
- Presentation is modest and age-appropriate: practical covered dress, no fan service framing in novel, comic, or anime.
- Violence stays off-focus for people: enemy deaths happen off-frame or by implication; slimes incapacitate, never consume on the page.
- Team Carrot Juice wins the Ember castle battle. Kids stay sidelined as present, supporting, witnessing. Orange lands no deciding blow.
- Ember capital becomes Carrot City / Carrot Castle (CC) after conquest.
- Grow plus Heal burns corruption. This is established in Chapters Ten and Eleven and enables the Dark Knight fall.
- Mentors hold agency from Chapter 10 onward. They direct. Orange discovers within their frame.
- Schooling is basic theory-only (reading, numbers, lore, magic theory). No kid casting. Family work is the real training: farm, forge, wood, lake.
- Stat Favor (FAV) replaces CHA / Luck / Blessing.
- Slime tendencies are SAS-driven movement and comfort only, never attack spells. Temporary splits divide reserves while out, dry out if spent, return if mana remains.
- Kingship is rare any-color tier: very old, fed colony, abandoned-forest conditions, slime level 50. Overeat bare land like virus and they dry up; nourished land lets them rest. King Shell is the limiter: egg-like harden outside, moist inside, fully vulnerable, disturbed fails, never auto. No accidental kings in Volumes 1–3.

## Proposed next, not locked

- Ylila becomes a mini fortress-city first. Log walls plus tended farmland.
- Tended farmland acts as a work domain. It aids efficiency, recovery, and mastery while working. It grants no free stats.
- John anchors Ylila. Orange stays mobile between Ylila and Carrot Castle.
- Guild outpost grows toward a fuller branch. Second classes stay exam-gated. More jobs exist for people and bonded slimes.
- Carrot land-heal starts with survey. Orange, John, Slime King, and volunteers restore waterways and fields. Poison reads as symptom. True origin stays unknown.
- Ember generals split. One surrenders to keep order. One flees with loyalists. Third stays unnamed.
- Team stays 4 kids for now. No 5th hero yet. Distant schools, cults, guilds, and flight stay far-future notes only.
