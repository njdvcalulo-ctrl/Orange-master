# Villager Fill-Up List

Per villager. Copy this:

1. Name, age, home zone
2. Job and output: what they make, how much, who uses it
3. Family link and daily beat
4. One-line mind: quiet, blunt, kind, stubborn
5. One habit or gag for scenes
6. Vol 1 use: feast, lesson, repair, patrol, barn, market
7. Limit: what they cannot do, who they call for help
8. Voice: one locked line if they speak often
