## B. Canon gaps (recommended fills)

### 1. Livelihood calendar (SEASONAL, locked shape)
- Dry (mango season): harvest weeks → Mango Fiestival (prep day + feast day).
- Wet (rice season): planting and tending; John leads, Orange on field duty.
- Cool (Amihan): kamatis and leafy beds, lake fishing peak, repair season
  (Ethan + Vincent catch up on tools, boats, houses).
- Each season opens with the same beat: strain outside → return → rest/meal
  → process → go out. The calendar IS the core loop.

### 2. Rites (quiet, subtle faith)
- Shade rest: travelers and workers pause under the Great Mango at noon.
  No prayer required, gratitude allowed. This is where John + Edward began.
- Pre-harvest thanks: Marie leads a short thanks before the first mango
  picked. Serra's blessing at the feast is the only formal one.
- Hymn night: mango hymn sung at the inn whenever a team returns safely.
  Team Mango-Tango drinks mango juice with the kids. No alcohol for minors.

### 3. Founding + past threats
- Founding (LOCKED): John claimed the land, Edward stayed for the shade.
  Mango shade founding already in the map doc.
- Past threats (PROPOSED): one golem probe from the west (repelled by Edward
  alone — the village never saw the fight, only the fixed fence); one brutal
  dry season (founding of the shared barns and Ben's logbook habit). Small,
  past, human-scale. Nothing that upstages Volume 1.

### 4. Off-village surplus rules
- Monthly surplus run to Ember market: John + Ben + one guard (Edward early,
  later a guild escort quest for the kids to observe, never to lead).
- 10% of surplus price returns as village share (school, barn, dock upkeep).
- Silver appears only here and in guild payouts. All Ylila prices stay copper.
- Road-south danger is why the run is monthly, not weekly.

### 5. Council + succession
- Council: Chief Edward + Elder Ruel + one head per working family (John for
  farms, Steve for lake, Ethan for forge, Vincent for wood, Teresa for inn).
- Rule: decisions by talk until all agree or Edward calls it. No votes shown
  on page; unity matters more than procedure for young readers.
- Succession: by deed, not blood. Edward names no heir. The council confirms
  whoever the village already follows. (Future hook, never Volume 1.)

### 6. Goblins + tame rules
- Goblins: forest-edge low tier, nuisance tier with horned rabbits. Drive
  off, never kill on page (violence stays off-focus). First real coordination
  for the kids, per the Crop Trouble event.
- What is a monster: human-like or humanoid = people — never tameable,
  never pets. Everything else non-humanoid = monster or animal. Livestock
  (rabbits, rock birds, furry pigs) are animals: tame by nature, kept in
  barns. Slimes are monsters: tameable by nothing, bondable by care (SAS).
- Taming (LOCKED meaning): a monster that lives and works with people is
  tame. Reference term: monster-bonded (a befriended monster). The word on
  the street stays taming. Requirements: time together, consistent feeding,
  shared survival or combat, care with intent.
- Reads as master-pet: Orange provides, slimes follow and obey. Runs as
  partnership: neither dominates — the user keeps control, the slime keeps
  its will. That is why it is bonding, not breaking.
- Food did the taming — yes. Orange's meals carry the growth signal, and
  the daily feeding is what built the bond. But food alone never suffices:
  the same meals without the relationship are just food (locked — enemies
  cannot replicate it). Time mattered because every meal carried care.
- Team Orange met every requirement without trying, plus the evolution
  extras (sustained support + growth-signal food), which is why bonding AND
  evolution came easy.
- Grow is NOT a taming spell (rejected by author, locked). Grow only makes
  training cleaner. Bonding comes from care, never from a spell.
- Formal Tamer class stays separate: guild-sanctioned, control-based,
  Ember-side exams. Orange holds no Tamer class. Villagers call what he does
  taming; the System reads it as bonding.
- Xavier hook (LOCKED direction): Xavier sees Orange meeting taming
  requirements effortlessly and gets very interested — his hydra dream now
  has a living example. Stays a dream through Volume 2; unlock story is
  Volume 3 if approved.
- Wild rumor (LOCKED as belief): wild monsters can't be tamed — or so they
  thought. No pet monsters in Volume 1 all the same.
- Goblins count as human-like under this rule, so they get the same
  protection: driven off, never killed on page. Befriending one later stays
  possible — and would break Ylila's brain in the best way.
- Slime policy: bonded slimes are family and friends (Fiestival made it
  public). Wild blues are left alone. Grime is burned out, never kept.
