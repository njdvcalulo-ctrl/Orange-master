# Chapter Prep

When the user says /chapterprep, prepare the next chapter. Do not write the chapter in this turn. Present the outline, stop, and wait for approval.

## Determine the next chapter

Check `docs/story/volume1-outline.md` for the chapter order and locks. Check `docs/manuscript/volume-1/` for what already exists. The next chapter is the first outlined one with no finished manuscript draft. If unclear, state the candidates and pick the most likely one.

## Research before outlining

Read the governing notes fresh each time:

- `docs/story/volume1-outline.md` for act placement and locked beats.
- `docs/story/ylila-tone-style.md` for voice, viewpoint, and fight rules.
- `docs/story-draft/revision-notes.md` for canon fixes that constrain the draft.
- The character files for everyone appearing, especially locked voice notes.
- The previous manuscript chapter for continuity.
- Past user critiques in this session: plain first intros for every new thing, no named skills or systems before they exist, no lore shorthand a new reader would trip on.

## Deliver the outline

Present beats in story order with rough word targets. Total target is 1,300–1,500 words unless the outline demands otherwise. Name which locked beats each section serves. List non-goals: things this chapter must not introduce or resolve.

## Offer options with pros and cons

For every material choice (openings, scene order, who appears, where a beat lands), give the options with one pro and one con each. Keep each to one short line. Recommend one and say why in one sentence. If a choice breaks an outline lock, flag it plainly.

## Then stop

End by asking which options the user picks. Do not draft, edit, or commit anything until they approve.
